# Tester kit: the closed test (B9)

Everything you send to closed testers, in English and Hindi, plus the feedback form questions. Written 27 Oct 2026 for build 0.3.8.

- **Dates:** the closed test opens **Mon 9 Nov**. At least **12 testers must be opted in by Mon 16 Nov** and stay opted in for **14 days in a row** (personal Play account). Recruit 20. The messages ask testers to stay until at least Mon 30 Nov, so a tester who joins on the last day still counts for 14 days.
- **Testers need:** a Gmail account on their phone and a 64-bit Android phone. Android Go phones may not see the app.
- **Placeholders** to replace before sending: `[GROUP LINK]`, `[OPT-IN LINK]`, `[FORM LINK]`, `[YOUR NAME]`.
- The Hindi texts are on the native-speaker review sheet ([../hindi-review/hindi-review.md](../hindi-review/hindi-review.md), group "Tester kit"). Use them after that check, or now if you are happy with them.

## 1. Set-up (you, about 30 min, after the internal test works)

**Google Group** (groups.google.com › Create group)

- Name "Dhaba Rush testers". Group email, for example `dhaba-rush-testers@googlegroups.com`.
- Privacy: who can search for the group: Anyone on the web. Who can join: **Anyone can join**. Who can view conversations: Group members. Who can post: Group managers only (no spam).
- Copy the group's link. That is `[GROUP LINK]`.

**Play Console** (Test and release › Testing › Closed testing › Manage track)

- Countries: India. Testers tab › Google Groups › add the group email › Save.
- **Feedback URL or email address:** `[FORM LINK]` (section 5).
- After the release is reviewed, the Testers tab shows "Join on the web". That is `[OPT-IN LINK]`. It should be `https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.littletagartstudios.dhabarush`.
- Optional: Settings › License testing › add testers who should test purchases. Everyone else must not buy anything: a closed-test purchase is real money.

**Google Form** (forms.google.com › Blank form)

- Title "Dhaba Rush test: your feedback / आपकी राय". Questions from section 5.
- Settings › Responses: **Collect email addresses: Do not collect**. Allow more than one response per person.
- Send › link icon › Shorten URL. That is `[FORM LINK]`.

## 5. Feedback form (Google Forms, 10 questions)

Questions only, in both languages, ready to paste. "Required" questions are marked. Don't ask for names, phone numbers or emails: the form stays anonymous.

| # | Question (English) | Question (Hindi) | Type | Options (English) | Options (Hindi) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| 4 | In the first 10 minutes, did you know what to do next? | पहले 10 मिनट में क्या आपको पता था कि आगे क्या करना है? | Multiple choice (required) | Always / Most of the time / Often confused / Lost | हमेशा / ज़्यादातर / अक्सर उलझन / समझ नहीं आया |
