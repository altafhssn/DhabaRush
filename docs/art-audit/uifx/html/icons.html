Dhaba Rush: every icon the player sees (3 Nov 2026 screenshots)

Left picture: the icon at 2x (for inspection). Right picture: phone size, i.e. the 720 px canvas shown on a 360 px wide phone (0.5x), rendered at 2x device pixels as a phone would. Source: screenshots in scratchpad/qa/slots/uxcap/shots. All icons are code-drawn (scripts/v3/ui/ui_icon.gd) or painted at run time (scripts/v3/world/dish_art.gd); the game ships no icon image files.
