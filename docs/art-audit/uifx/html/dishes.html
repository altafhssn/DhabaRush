Dhaba Rush: the 32 dish pictures at the sizes the game shows them

Redrawn from scripts/v3/world/dish_art.gd (same shapes and colours, drawn as vectors instead of texels: a faithful stand-in, not a game capture). Sizes are canvas px on the 720 x 1280 canvas: 96 = the source image, 52 = the product card on a counter sheet (104 px), 22 = the NEW ON THE MENU card (44 px), 18 = a menu chip (36 px), 14.5 = one dish in a traveller's order bubble (29 px). Each is shown at phone size (a 360 px wide phone shows the canvas at 0.5x), rendered at 2x device pixels as a phone would. Coloured borders mark the groups that are hard to tell apart.
A: grey glass on a grey dish: chai, gur chai, coffee, chaasB: the kulhad three times (thandai = kesar doodh pixel for pixel)C: six spotted discsD: steel glass with a cream dome, three timesE: one steel katori, five fillingsF: baati balls on a steel plate, both counters
