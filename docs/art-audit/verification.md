# Adversarial check of the four art audits (Dhaba Rush) — verdicts

Read-only. Nothing under the project was touched, Godot was not run. Everything was re-measured with my own scripts in `docs/art-audit/verification-work/` (`glbtool.py` glb reader/comparer, `pngtool.py` zlib PNG reader + WCAG/CIELAB, `paltool.py` palette parser, `deep.py`/`deep2.py`/`deep3.py` geometry diffs; outputs `tris.csv`, `cmp.txt`). Python = Blender 5.2's python3.13 `-I -B`.

Score: 9 confirmed, 5 corrected, 0 fully refuted. The two corrections that matter most for the owner: (13) the "six stale models" finding is mostly wrong, and (10) the tandoor clones are worse than stated (two Bablus, no Munna).

## Contradictions between the four files

1. **Palette near-pairs.** Scene 1.7 and S13 say "23 pairs within dE 7"; the scene auditor's own `work/palette_check.txt` line 2 says "dE<7: 41"; pipeline says 20 under 5 / 59 under 8. Mine: 41 under 7, 20 under 5, 59 under 8. The "23" in the scene text is a typo.
2. **Draw calls.** Models M8 "400-450"; pipeline 1.4 quotes the measured 190 (`docs/optimise/report.md:297`). Use 190.
3. **Tangents.** Models M10 recommends turning `ensure_tangents` off; `docs/optimise/report.md:294` says that was tried and **rejected** (darkened ~75 % of world pixels by 3-10/255, reverted), and pipeline 1.4 says "leave". M10's suggestion should be dropped.
4. **Jassi.** Characters C1 "Jassi is Munna" vs the code (both tandoor hires are Bablu, see claim 10). Models M5/M6 do not contradict; they just never reach it.
5. **PA2 vs PA3.** Pipeline lists street_v3 under "geometry differs" and separately says rebuilds reorder indices; the first is caused by the second (claim 13).
6. Consistent where it should be: palette import counts (models 67/83 shipped, pipeline 69/115 total: same data); 113 shipped / 106 loaded / 7 unused; waiter shirt colours (scene's #EC9C2C and #FEBF2E are SAFFRON and DAL under the day light, as characters C3 says); 6-7 mesh parts per person; hoarding 948 tris.
7. Minor wording: models M1 says the 30 un-imported files "are the whole MENU_MODELS list" while M1 itself says MENU_MODELS has 59 ids; it means "all are in that list".

## Suggested changes that touch a project rule

- **No windowed Godot:** models M1 and pipeline PA1 (and their ranked items 2 and 3) say "open the project in the Godot editor once". That is a windowed run. It should read "the owner opens the editor, or a headless import like the QA kit's `tools/testkit/qa/import.sh`".
- **Owner approval before anything ships:** pipeline PA2 ("rebuild once and have the owner approve the six") and models M2/M5 (re-export all glbs, delete files) change what ships; they say "owner approves", keep it that way. No audit asks for an APK/iOS export; pipeline explicitly declines to export. Good.
- **Hindi frozen / no i18n edits:** scene S16's painted rate board ("CHAI 10 · THALI 60" via Label3D) and S1(b)'s "repaint the pump-set ad" add or change in-world text. Baked board art is "never translated" (`hoarding_node.gd:16`), so it is not an i18n edit, but if the new Label3D strings go through `tr()` they become Hindi-gated strings. Flag before doing.
- **Tutorial beats unchanged:** scene S1(a) (shift `HOME_OFFSET`, `camera_rig.gd:70`) moves where the tutorial's tap targets land on screen; the auditor does say to run the tutorial suite. S14's sun-yaw change is light only. Characters C1's "drop bablu/munna from the tandoor list" is safe.

## Things the audits missed (found while checking)

1. **Two Bablus at the tandoor** (claim 10) — the clone problem is slightly worse than C1 says, and the "munna" entry in `layout3.gd:843` is dead.
2. **`scenery.glb` is the one file whose rebuild really changes triangle count** (5,908 → 5,916). It is excluded from export, so no player impact, but it shows the dead files are not even stable.
3. **The NH 48 map recolours KURTA** (a cloth swatch, 38 uses) to sandstone `E8B98A` (`make_palette_variant.py:36`) although its docstring says cloth is unchanged. Only the three world meshes take the variant (`region_look.gd`), so people keep their kurtas, but any KURTA-coloured cloth baked into dhaba_v3 / street_v3 / family_hall (decor charpai sheets, for example) turns sandstone in NH 48. Worth one look in a capture.
4. **`fairy_wire_*` nodes are outside the FairyLights driver** (prefix `fairy_bulb_` only). Hiding wires by day, if wanted, needs its own toggle.
5. The second yellow waiter in the home shot (405,640) is closer to the light tile (1.09) than the one the scene audit quoted (1.48): S2 is, if anything, understated.
6. `.godot/imported` keeps both a stale 2,580-B lossless `.ctex` and the `etc2`/`s3tc` 16,452-B copies for each of the 69 flipped palettes; on Android only the `etc2` copy ships (`project.godot` `import_etc2_astc=true`), so models M2's 1,149,772-B figure is the right one for the APK.
