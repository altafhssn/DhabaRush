2. What this policy covers

This policy covers the game Dhaba Rush: Indian Idle Tycoon on Android, distributed through Google Play. It explains what data the game and the services built into it handle, why, and the choices you have. It does not cover Google Play itself, Google's own services or other websites; their own privacy policies apply. The game is not yet available on iPhone or iPad; we will update this policy before it is.

3. Data kept on your phone

The game saves the following in its private storage on your phone. None of it is sent to us.

  • Game progress: in-game cash and coins, upgrades, staff, buildings, tasks, tutorial progress, how many times you have opened the game, and when you last played (used to calculate offline earnings).
  • Settings: music, sound, vibration, language, number format, graphics quality, your analytics answer (yes or no, and when you gave it) and your Player ID, a random code made on your phone (it is sent to GameAnalytics only while analytics is on).
  • Purchases: whether you own VIP or No Ads, and a record of the purchase tokens of delivered consumable items, so an item is never delivered twice.
  • Service data: the ad and analytics services keep their own small files on your phone, for example your ad consent choice (in regions where Google asks for it) and, if you allowed analytics, events waiting to be sent.

The game does not back up this data to the cloud. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in Android Settings, deletes all of it, including your progress. Purchases of VIP and No Ads come back automatically when you reinstall with the same Google account.

4. Data collected when you play

The game never asks you to grant a permission, and it has no access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files, phone number or calls. It only has permissions that Android grants without asking: internet access, network state (to know whether you are online), vibration, Google Play billing and the advertising ID. The ad software and Android libraries built into the game add a few more of the same kind: basic phone state (for example the type of network connection, never your phone number or calls) and short background tasks (keeping the phone awake briefly to finish them).

4.1 Ads: Google AdMob

The game shows ads from Google AdMob (Google LLC; Google Ireland Limited for users in the EEA and Switzerland). There are two kinds:

  • Rewarded videos that you choose to watch for an in-game reward.
  • Full-screen ads between moments of play (never during the tutorial). Buying No Ads removes these; rewarded videos stay available if you want them.

To show, measure and protect ads, Google's ad software in the game collects, automatically:

  • your phone's advertising ID and app set ID;
  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general (approximate) location;
  • device and app information such as phone model, operating system, language and app version;
  • interactions such as app launches, ad views, taps and video completions;
  • diagnostic information about how the app and the ad software perform.

Google uses this data to serve ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and abuse and, where allowed, show personalised ads based on your interests. We ask Google to show only ads suitable for teens and older. How Google uses this data is explained in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Consent. Each time the game starts, it asks Google's consent service whether a consent question applies where you are (this request sends your IP address and app information to Google). In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and anywhere else Google requires it, Google's consent form appears before any ad is requested, and no ads load until you answer. In those regions Settings shows a PRIVACY OPTIONS button so you can change your choice at any time. In India and other regions where no consent form is required, ads may be personalised unless you turn this off on your phone (see Your choices).

4.2 Game analytics: GameAnalytics

We use GameAnalytics (GameAnalytics ApS, Pilestræde 58, 1112 Copenhagen K, Denmark) to understand how the game is played, balance it and fix problems. GameAnalytics works for us as a service provider (data processor).

Only with your permission. The first time you open the game, a short card ("Help us improve") explains what is sent and asks whether you agree. Until you answer, nothing is sent to GameAnalytics and GameAnalytics is not started. If you tap No thanks, nothing is ever sent to GameAnalytics and the game plays exactly the same. If you tap Allow, analytics starts. You can change your answer at any time with the ANALYTICS switch in the game's Settings; when you turn it off, sending stops at once. If you allow it, GameAnalytics receives:

  • your Player ID: a random code created on your phone and shown at the bottom of the game's Settings (it is not linked to your name, email or Google account), and, where your phone makes them available, the advertising ID and app set ID;
  • your IP address, which GameAnalytics uses to work out your country;
  • device information: phone model and manufacturer, operating system version, language setting, connection type, app version;
  • session information: when a session starts and ends, how long it lasts, and how many sessions you have played;
  • gameplay events: for example tutorial steps, upgrades and staff bought, buildings built, in-game rewards, whether an ad was offered, watched, skipped or failed to load, and whether a purchase was started, completed, cancelled or failed;
  • for a completed purchase: the item, its price and currency (never your payment details);
  • automatic error reports when something goes wrong in the game.

The game never sends your name, contacts, messages or precise location. See the GameAnalytics privacy notice.

4.3 In-app purchases: Google Play

Purchases (for example VIP or No Ads) are handled by Google Play's billing system. Google collects and processes your payment details under the Google Privacy Policy; we never see your card, UPI or bank details. From Google Play the game receives information about the purchase itself: which item, the order ID, a purchase token, the time and the purchase state. We use it to deliver the item, confirm the purchase to Google, restore items you own after a reinstall and prevent double delivery. If you allowed analytics, the item, price and currency of completed purchases are also sent to GameAnalytics (section 4.2) for sales statistics.

4.4 When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write, and use them only to answer you and keep a record of your request.

5. What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address (unless you write to us), phone number, postal address, contacts, photos, videos, audio, files, messages, precise location, browsing history, health data, or financial details such as card, UPI or bank account numbers. The game has no chat and no way to share anything with other players. We do not collect "sensitive personal data or information" as defined in India's IT (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011.

Google and GameAnalytics process data on servers outside India, including in the United States and the European Union. Under India's DPDP Act, personal data may be transferred outside India except to countries the Government of India restricts.

9. How long data is kept

  • On your phone: until you uninstall the game or clear its storage.
  • GameAnalytics: for a limited period under its retention schedule (at the time of writing, at most 18 months depending on the kind of data), after which it is deleted or anonymised. We can also ask GameAnalytics to delete data earlier (section 11).
  • Google: under Google's own retention policies, described in the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Emails to us: for as long as we need to deal with your request and then for up to [12 months], unless the law requires us to keep them longer.

10. Your choices

  • Ad personalisation and the advertising ID: in Android Settings open Google > Ads (on some phones Privacy > Ads) and choose Delete advertising ID or Reset advertising ID. You can also manage ads through Google My Ad Center.
  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions where Google asks): change or withdraw your choice at any time with PRIVACY OPTIONS in the game's Settings.
  • Full-screen ads: buy No Ads. Rewarded videos are always optional.
  • Analytics: answer No thanks on the first-launch card, or turn ANALYTICS off in the game's Settings at any time. From then on nothing more is sent to GameAnalytics. Turning it back on starts it again with the same Player ID. To ask us to delete analytics data that was already sent, see section 11.
  • Everything else: uninstall the game, or clear its storage. This stops all collection by the game and resets your Player ID.

11. Deleting your data

  1. On your phone: uninstall Dhaba Rush, or open Android Settings > Apps > Dhaba Rush > Storage > Clear storage. This deletes your progress and the IDs the game created.
  2. Analytics already sent: email info@littletagartstudios.com with the subject "Dhaba Rush: delete my data". We hold no name or email that links you to your data, so please include your Player ID: it is shown at the bottom of the game's Settings ("Player ID: XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX"); tap it to copy it, then paste it into your email. If you cannot open the game any more, send your advertising ID if you have not reset it (Android Settings > Google > Ads), your phone model and roughly when you played. We will ask GameAnalytics to delete the data filed under that Player ID and confirm to you within 30 days. Do not uninstall before writing to us if you want us to find your data: uninstalling deletes the Player ID from your phone. If you never allowed analytics, nothing was sent to GameAnalytics and there is nothing there to delete.
  3. Ad data: Google holds this under its own policies. Reset or delete your advertising ID (section 10) and use Google's tools at Google Account > Data & privacy.
  4. Purchases: Google Play keeps its own order records, and the law requires transaction records to be kept for a period; we cannot delete those. Deleting your data does not cancel items you own on your Google account.

If we cannot identify your data, it is still deleted or anonymised automatically at the end of the retention period in section 9.

12. Your rights in India

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 (as their provisions come into force), you have the right to:

  • get a summary of the personal data we process about you, what we do with it, and who we have shared it with;
  • have your personal data corrected, completed, updated or erased;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, as easily as you gave it, for analytics with the ANALYTICS switch in Settings (this does not affect processing that already happened);
  • have your grievances addressed by our Grievance Officer;
  • nominate another person to exercise these rights for you if you die or become unable to do so.

To use any of these rights, email info@littletagartstudios.com. We will answer within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

13. Your rights in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

If the GDPR or a similar law applies to you, you have the right to access your personal data, have it corrected or erased, restrict or object to its processing, receive it in a portable format, and withdraw consent at any time (use PRIVACY OPTIONS in Settings for ads and the ANALYTICS switch in Settings for analytics). Email us to use these rights, with your Player ID for analytics data; we answer within one month. You can also complain to the data protection authority where you live or work.

14. Children

Dhaba Rush is meant for players aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you are under 18, please play only with the permission of a parent or guardian. The game does not ask for anyone's age, so it cannot tell how old a player is. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child under 13 has played the game, or you want us to delete data about your child, write to info@littletagartstudios.com and we will delete what we can identify (section 11). Parents can also turn off ad personalisation on a child's phone (section 10).

15. Security

Data sent by the ad and analytics services travels encrypted over the internet (TLS). Data on your phone is stored in the game's private storage, which other apps cannot read. We run no servers of our own that store your data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we choose established providers and collect as little as we can.
