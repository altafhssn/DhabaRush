# What idle-tycoon players want and what they complain about

Research date: 31 Oct 2026. Scope: hands-off idle tycoons (Idle Supermarket Tycoon, Idle Theme Park Tycoon, Idle Miner Tycoon, Idle Restaurant Tycoon: Empire, Eatventure, Pizza Ready!, Roadside Empire, Travel Center Tycoon, Idle Food Bar: Olivia & Oliver).

Labels: **[M]** measured fact from a store listing or report; **[R]** player review/opinion, quoted verbatim; **[E]** third-party estimate (Sensor Tower, AppMagic, aggregator NLP); **[I]** my inference.

Sourcing. App Store quotes: apps.apple.com (dated) or JustUseApp mirrors (page date given, reviews undated). Google Play quotes: Play pages fetched 31 Oct 2026 with the India locale (`gl=IN`, `hl=en`/`hl=hi`); recent-first, dates not captured, treat as 2024-26. Reddit: one Jan 2026 mirror snapshot plus search snippets of an Aug 2024 thread.

## 1. Top 10 things players praise

**1. Ads only when I choose, and they pay out.** The single most-repeated praise.
- "Finally! An idle game with all the aspects I love! No forced ads AT ALL" (Eatventure, App Store via JustUseApp, page 19 Sep 2026) [R]
- "weirdly I've probably watched 500+ ads by now and I'm not mad because it was my choice. I wish other games would realize this." (Eatventure, Google Play) [R]
- "I love how adds are optional and how you still get money even if you aren't on" (Idle Supermarket, App Store via JustUseApp, Aug 2026) [R]
- Kolibri (Mar 2021): ads ~50% of revenue; best placement "double offline earnings on return" [M, company].

**2. Relaxing, de-stressing, "before bed".**
- "It has become a must play before bed time now as a way to de stress from the day. A real diamond." (Eatventure, GP) [R]
- "I like to play it to help me relax and get sleepy at night instead of scrolling socials." (Eatventure, GP) [R]
- "I wanted to give 5 star for this calm and peace game to play." (Travel Center Tycoon, GP) [R]

**3. Fits any session length without penalty.**
- "I can play intensely for 5 minutes or an hour, then put it down without sacrificing my position in the game." (Eatventure, GP) [R]
- r/incremental_games, Aug 2024: a good idle is "something you can play and progress in with small amounts of playtime" [R]
- GameAnalytics (Mar 2025): idle avg session 8 min, 5.3 sessions/day [E].

**4. Money waiting when you come back (offline earnings).**
- "I enjoy expanding different sections of the store and earning money even while offline." (Idle Supermarket, GP) [R]
- "It was nice to have a game I could play offline on my lunch break." (Idle Restaurant Tycoon, GP) [R]

**5. Watching the place visibly grow.**
- "It was so fun watching your theme park grow more and more every day." (Idle Theme Park, GP) [R]
- "the graphics and progression are satisfying." (Eatventure, GP) [R]

**6. Upgrades that change the scene, not just a number.**
- "Lots of upgrades, plus when you upgrade it's not just a stat boost" (Idle Supermarket, App Store via JustUseApp) [R]
- "It has so many things to unlock on one ride and it so many different rides to advance and unlock." (Idle Theme Park, App Store, 28 Jan 2023) [R]
- "I love earning enhancement cards." (Idle Restaurant Tycoon, GP) [R]

**7. Charm: art, music, humour.**
- "the music, sound effects, and food graphics, great!" (Eatventure, GP) [R]
- "there's a lot of humor woven into the upgrades (\"Hire Mike's friend Mike, +1 Chef\")" (Eatventure, GP) [R]
- "it's lovely to look at, animations with people and rides etc are great" (Idle Theme Park, GP) [R]

**8. Simple to understand.**
- "I like the simplicity of the gameplay and the controls." (Eatventure, GP) [R]
- "Concept is easy, gameplay is smooth." (Roadside Empire, GP via search snippet) [R]
- "its easy for kids to play." (Idle Miner, GP) [R]

**9. Honest: the game is what the ad showed.**
- "Its nice to have a game that lives up to it's ads" (Eatventure, GP) [R]
- "I've gotta give it props for actually being what it claims to be and not an alliance survival game in disguise." (Eatventure, GP) [R]

**10. Fair F2P and things to come back for (events, renovate, clubs).**
- "You don't have to watch ads or spend money to progress (I haven't spent anything and rarely watch ads)." (Eatventure, GP) [R]
- "the weekly challenges are fun." (Eatventure, GP, one-year player) [R]
- "The renovate feature makes this game much better than others like it." (Idle Restaurant Tycoon, GP) [R]

## 2. Top 10 complaints

**1. Forced/interstitial ads and ad frequency.** The most common 1-star reason.
- "the ads come every 20 seconds and last anywhere from 5-10 seconds each" (Pizza Ready!, App Store, 17 Feb 2025) [R]
- "after i got a few days in they started just poping up randomly. And i mean it was one after another every thirty seconds. You could tell they were pushing the ad free version." (Travel Center Tycoon, GP) [R]
- "Feels like I'm playing just to watch ads!" (Idle Restaurant Tycoon, GP) [R]

**2. Ad tech that breaks: no reward, app reload, Play Store redirect.**
- "1 out of 3 ads don't give rewards after watching." (Idle Supermarket, GP) [R]
- "ads keep restarting the game all the time ... having to restart it ten times In twenty minutes is too much." (Idle Miner, GP) [R]
- "if you don't push the x in the exact right spot, it opens a browser window AND Google Play Store ... makes you miss out on your reward 90% of the time." (Idle Restaurant Tycoon, GP) [R]

**3. Pop-up and offer spam on launch.**
- "I logged in after not playing for some time and copped 13 game pop ups in one hit." (Idle Miner, GP) [R]
- "too many icons on the screen. I just want to play. ... uninstalled" (Travel Center Tycoon, GP) [R]
- "Wish when you at least buy the Ad Free Version that you can TOGGLE the premium offers OFF." (Idle Miner, GP) [R]

**4. Hard gates that read as paywalls (manager cards, research tokens, vault caps).**
- "they changed it by adding managers you have to level up in order to proceed. It's basically impossible to progress at a certain point without paying." (Idle Supermarket, GP) [R]
- "To get the next stall based on my current earnings it would take 120 hours of constant play (yes I've worked that out with a calculator)" (Idle Supermarket, GP) [R]
- "Once your vault reaches lvl10 that's it you can't progress anything" (Travel Center Tycoon, GP; multiple reviews say this has been unfixed "since July 2022") [R]

**5. Updates that reset, nerf or rebalance what players earned.**
- "but hey let's give everyone 700 gold and reset years of gameplay. pathetic. I've had it for almost 9 years. time to let go." (Idle Supermarket, GP) [R]
- "STOP EXPERIMENTING WITH THE GAME. Its fine as it is. If you have to, then introduce new supermarkets" (Idle Supermarket, GP, 2-year player) [R]
- "I hate the new card collecting system. I don't really like when games introduce new things that decrease value for the players." (Idle Food Bar, App Store, 6 May 2025) [R]

**6. Content drought: "nothing to do", repetition.**
- "once you complete one story there is nothing else to do." (Roadside Empire, App Store via JustUseApp) [R]
- "same food, same dishes, every other country, it gets tiring." (Eatventure, GP) [R]
- "Something new, truly new would be nice ... Every other game I play updates often or regularly. This one has had nothing." (Idle Restaurant Tycoon, App Store, 14 Apr 2022) [R]

**7. Wait walls and slow late game.**
- "after about 15 min of playing you get the point where all you can do is wait. Open the ap for 5 sec to click on a new build then wait 3hrs" (Travel Center Tycoon, GP) [R]
- "Now, it takes a week just to unlock the next ride." (Idle Theme Park, GP) [R]
- "the 3 hours that it takes to upgrade the shop and multiply profits, absolutely ridiculous." (Idle Supermarket, GP) [R]

**8. Lost progress, no cloud save.**
- "it went back to the very beginning with the tutorial which completely wiped out my progress." (Idle Restaurant Tycoon, App Store, 23 Jan 2021) [R]
- "There's no cloud system for the game so there's no way to retrieve progress." (Idle Restaurant Tycoon, App Store, 21 Jan 2021) [R]
- "The thing that solely broke the game however was logging in last night to find my progress gone." (Idle Restaurant Tycoon, GP) [R]

**9. Bugs, freezes, battery, server dependency.**
- "it freezes like every 10-20 minutes" (Idle Supermarket, 21 Mar 2026) [R]
- "I've never seen a game that pops up so often \"Waiting for server response\"" (Idle Theme Park, GP) [R]
- Hindi: "बार बार बंद हो जाता है ... क्रैश हो जा रहा है" ("keeps closing again and again ... crashing") (Idle Miner, GP hi-IN) [R]

**10. Too shallow / nothing to decide (the optimizer's complaint).**
- "there are no recurring costs, which makes the game extremely shallow" (Idle Supermarket, App Store via JustUseApp) [R]
- "Upgrade simulator" — lacks "decisions, risk, and meaningful numbers" (Eatventure, ComplaintsBoard) [R]
- "realize that the game is just playing itself it isn't worth it ... Just means uninstall faster." (Idle Miner, GP) [R]

Below the top 10: **misleading ads** ("next time make your ads a little more accurate, devs." Eatventure; Lancaric survey, Dec 2025: 75% uninstall immediately when the game does not match the ad [E, paywalled]); **adult ad content** ("PARENT ALERT: Inappropriate Ads", Eatventure); **new-location resets** ("nothing you did in the previous area matters ... feels very punishing", Eatventure GP); **IAP not delivered** (Idle Theme Park, Idle Food Bar, Idle Supermarket, 2025-26); **confusing** ("very fun. But it's confusing !!", Roadside Empire; hidden costs "never mentioned in advance", Idle Restaurant Tycoon).

## 3. What makes players quit: session 1, day 1, week 1

**Session 1 (first 5-15 min).** [R unless noted]
- Ads before trust: "I didn't even play 5 minutes of this game before I closed it and deleted it. I had to watch 3 ads." (Pizza Ready!, Jan 2025); "A full-length ad plays before the game loads" (Eatventure complaint).
- A wait wall inside the first minutes: "Right from the start you have to wait an hour just to build anything or use what few gems you have." (Travel Center Tycoon).
- Confusion at one step: Kolibri's Idle Restaurant soft launch (Oct 2020) found "many players got stuck at one early tutorial point" [M, company]. Its Idle Miner playtest (7 players, 3x15 min): players upgraded mines, ignored elevators/warehouses, hit a bottleneck and churned; contextual prompts fixed it [M, company].
- Low-end device failure: "mera 2 gb ram ka phone hai aur seconds bhi nahi chalte" ("my phone has 2 GB RAM and the seconds don't even tick") (Travel Center Tycoon, GP hi-IN); "I am stuck at the first Toilet construction. The timer stays at 57 seconds".
- Benchmarks: D1 21-24% average, <4% D30 (vgm.co, Sep 2026) [E]; top-10% idle D1 45.6% (GameAnalytics) [E]; Lumikai: "30% D1 and sub-10% D7" is insufficient to scale in India (Mar 2026) [E].

**Day 1.**
- The loop collapses into "open, spend, close": "The first five minutes are okay. Then you open the game once an hour ... play for 1 minute, spend all your money, and close again." (Travel Center Tycoon).
- Boredom when the second scenario looks like the first: "I played for a whole day. Had my fun. Got bored after drive through scenario." (Eatventure).
- A dev's targets after 7 prototypes (dev.to, Aug 2026): first upgrade <60 s, first automation <3 min, first prestige 10-15 min; "no prestige or endgame, so players drop off after the first session" [opinion].

**Week 1.**
- Interstitials switching on after N sessions: "after i got a few days in they started just poping up randomly" (Travel Center Tycoon).
- Running out of content: "Finished all available restaurants in a very short time" (Idle Restaurant Tycoon); "Unfortunately it gets boring by chapter 3." (Roadside Empire).
- A hard gate: "I made it all the way to Paris with no problem now I've been stuck there for almost a week now." (Idle Supermarket).
- Lost progress on reinstall/phone change (section 2.8).

**[I]** For Dhaba Rush: content ending at ~1-1.5 h sits exactly on the day-1/week-1 cliff these reviews describe; the fixes players name are a second location/prestige, a weekly event, and no interstitials until trust exists.

## 4. What keeps players for months

- Long-tenure voices: "I've been playing this everyday for the past year ... the weekly challenges are fun." (Eatventure, GP); "Been playing this game for 2 yrs now." and "I've had it for almost 9 years" (Idle Supermarket, GP); "I have played this game for years ... my favorite idle game to date." (Idle Theme Park, GP) [R].
- The retention mechanics named by those players: weekly events/challenges, a new city or park (prestige with carry-over), gear/pet/outfit collection ("I do love progression"), clubs (requested with chat and kicking), renovate/evolve of an existing location, customisation.
- Kolibri built Idle Restaurant Tycoon as GaaS: "Weekly time-limited event restaurants" plus new restaurants post-launch (Dec 2020) [M, company].
- AppQuantum's Idle Lumber Empire (Jun 2024): a live-ops plan after 2.5 years doubled downloads and raised spending 2.5x [E, company].
- Counter-signal: the same long-tenure players write "time to let go" when an update resets or gates them (2.5). **[I]** Tenure is trust; one bad rebalance spends it.

## 5. Cosy players vs optimizer players

Both exist in every review set; they ask for opposite screens.

**Cosy/relaxed** (majority of 5-star text): "calm and peace game", "before bed", "I just want to play", "just not the relaxing play that I'm looking for in an \"idle\" game" (Idle Miner, after pop-ups and "too many screens to navigate through"). They need: one obvious next action; a quiet screen (reviewers count icons and pop-ups); ambient life ("All of the character animations are idle, too. Chefs just stand there with a timer." is a complaint, Idle Restaurant Tycoon); no interruptions. Buergi (Replay, 2024): idle games qualify as cosy but monetisation "undermines the genre's calm appeal" [academic].

**Optimizers** (loud in 2-3 star text): "yes I've worked that out with a calculator"; "no recurring costs ... extremely shallow"; "lacks decisions, risk, and meaningful numbers"; "the developers do not know how to count past a trillion" (Eatventure, number formatting); r/incremental_games (Aug 2024): "number go up" matters less than "being able to influence the outcome with smart decisions"; numbers rising too fast is a turn-off; "a really important balance is active vs idle playtime" [R]. Alharthi et al. (CHI 2018): the genre moves players "from playing to planning" [academic]. They need: honest per-minute income; which station is the bottleneck right now; cost vs payoff on each upgrade; the offline cap stated; number formatting past 1e12; and, per the DEV post, progressive reveal (show the next 2-3 upgrades, not 20).

**Both watch the sim and judge it.** "the scripting is so bad for walking that big table 13 never gets cleaned" (Idle Restaurant Tycoon); "your moneymaker businesses were all stupidly designed to make the line wait for every car to leave" (Roadside Empire). **[I]** Dhaba Rush's waiter/seat bottleneck is exactly the kind of thing both groups will stare at; broken pathing becomes a 1-star review, legible bottlenecks become the game.

## 6. India-specific findings

- Market: non-RMG games $1.5B in CY25 (+17%); 555M gamers; payer conversion 25%; casual ARPPU $3; rewarded-ad eCPM $2.43 vs $1.10 aggregate (Lumikai via Outlook, 23 Mar 2026) [E]. Sensor Tower (Jun 2025): 8.45B downloads, ~$400M IAP in FY24-25; downloads "favour simple, familiar games"; Ludo King (1.25B downloads) cited as culturally resonant design [E].
- Audience: 44-45% women; 60-66% non-metro; 56% of payers buy within the first week; 47% "do not mind ads" (Lumikai, May/Nov 2025) [E]. Lightbox (Sep 2025): 12% find ads annoying, 38% would quit on excessive ad load [E].
- Language/theme: 34% of gamers prefer Hindi, 28% English (SCCG 2024, citing FICCI-EY Mar 2024) [E]; Lumikai 2021: "higher willingness to play games with vernacular and uniquely-Indian themes" [E, no %]. Hindi-locale Play reviews exist for every title and are blunt: "pay out ka लालच dette है और लोग फस जाते हैं" ("they bait you with payout and people get trapped") (Idle Supermarket, hi-IN).
- Devices/storage: Newzoo (Nov 2021): 92% of Indian smartphones have >=2 GB RAM, 53% >=4 GB [E]; SanDisk (2018): 62% run out of space every three months [E, old]. Kolibri's stock reply to Hindi reviews: "make sure you have enough free storage space on your phone" [M]. Competitor sizes (iOS): Idle Theme Park 908 MB, Idle Food Bar 455 MB, Pizza Ready 388 MB, Idle Restaurant 355 MB [M]. **[I]** A <150 MB APK is a differentiator.
- Offline: valued ("offline on my lunch break", Idle Restaurant); server dependency penalised ("Waiting for server response", Idle Theme Park); a Hindi review objects to "फालतू के नेट खराब करना" (wasting data) [R].
- Price points [M, Play IN listings]: IAP floors ₹24 (Idle Miner), ₹30 (Idle Restaurant), ₹58 (Eatventure), ₹89 (Codigames), ₹90 (Travel Center); ceilings ₹3,039-₹11,300; Play's India minimum is ₹10. Lumikai: ₹200-500 tickets go mostly to video/social [E]. On no-ads: "I would buy if it was $5 but $10 is too much for me." (Travel Center) [R]. **[I]** Rs 299 VIP x2 is above the genre's entry price; a Rs 49-99 first offer with Rs 299 as "x2 forever" (Idle Food Bar: $5.99) matches the listings better.

## 7. Ranked: what players of this genre look for

1. **Ads I choose, that always pay out.** "no forced ads AT ALL"; "I had to watch 3 ads" -> deleted in 5 min; "1 out of 3 ads don't give rewards".
2. **Respect for my time at any session length, with money waiting on return.** "5 minutes or an hour ... without sacrificing my position"; "earning money even while offline".
3. **A calm screen with one obvious next thing.** "I just want to play"; "13 game pop ups in one hit"; "too many screens to navigate".
4. **A place I can watch grow, with charm.** "watching your theme park grow more and more every day"; "Hire Mike's friend Mike, +1 Chef"; "animations with people and rides etc are great".
5. **Fair progress with no hard gate.** "impossible to progress at a certain point without paying"; "vault reaches lvl10 that's it".
6. **Upgrades that change what I see.** "when you upgrade it's not just a stat boost"; "so many things to unlock on one ride".
7. **Always a next place or event.** "nothing else to do" / "The story only last 6 levels" vs "the weekly challenges are fun"; "renovate ... doesn't feel like wasted time".
8. **Something to think about (bottlenecks, honest numbers).** "influence the outcome with smart decisions"; "no recurring costs ... extremely shallow"; "worked that out with a calculator".
9. **It never loses my stuff.** "wiped out my progress"; "no cloud system"; "freezes like every 10-20 minutes".
10. **Honesty: matches the ad, and updates never take away what I earned.** "lives up to it's ads"; "reset years of gameplay. pathetic."
