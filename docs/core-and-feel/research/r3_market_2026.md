# What idle tycoon players currently get: market snapshot, October 2026

Prepared 31 Oct 2026 for the Dhaba Rush team. Labels: **[M]** measured/official, **[E]** third-party estimate (Sensor Tower, AppMagic, Appodeal, etc.), **[R]** player review (verbatim), **[O]** analyst opinion, **[I]** my inference. Every fact carries its date and a source number from the list at the end. Unknown cells in the matrix are marked *unknown*, not guessed.

## 1. Who leads the genre in 2025-2026

**The market around idle.** Mobile IAP revenue was $81.75B in 2025 (+1.3%) while downloads fell 7.2% to 50.4B [E, Sensor Tower, Jan 2026] [S1][S2]. Mobile game ad revenue passed $12B in 2025; the share of games monetising with ads rose from 45.1% (Jul 2021) to 55.6% (May 2026) [E, Sensor Tower, Jul 2026] [S3]. Hybrid-casual was the only casual segment with real IAP growth: +20% to $4.2B [E, Sensor Tower] [S2]; AppMagic, with a narrower taxonomy, has hybrid-casual IAP going from $390M to $733M (+88%) through Oct 2025 [E] [S4]. "Simulation" inside Casual earned $1.9B (-4.8%), of which farming is half; "time management" (restaurant-style active sims) was $135M (-12%) on 509M downloads [E, AppMagic, Feb 2026] [S5].

**Idle sub-genre leaders (Sensor Tower weekly-peak lists, US).** Q1 2025 Android revenue: Gold & Goblins: Idle Merger (AppQuantum) ~$400K/week mid-Feb; The Tower - Idle Tower Defense $184K; Idle Zombie Miner $137K; Carnival Tycoon $77K; Idle Lumber Empire $48K [E] [S6]. Q3 2025 iOS revenue: Gold and Goblins $239K; Idle Zombie Miner $197K; The Tower $162K; Idle Lumber Empire $110K; Little Farm Story: Idle Tycoon (SayGames) $108K; downloads led by Idle Lumber Empire (43K/week) and Idle Zombie Miner (42K/week) [E] [S7]. Pattern [I]: the grossing idle leaders are merge-idle, tower-defence-idle and mining-idle, not restaurant idle; restaurant-themed idle wins on downloads, not revenue.

**Tycoon x coin-looter.** Top Tycoon (BeheFun) made $29.6M on 11.7M installs in 2025, the only 2025 release to enter the Coin Looter top 10 [E, AppMagic] [S5]; Carnival Tycoon is cited at $40M lifetime [E, AppMagic, Sep 2025] [S8].

**Downloads leader: Pizza Ready! (Supercent).** 150M downloads in 2025 alone, ~400M lifetime, "most-downloaded hybrid casual game globally in 2025"; Supercent became the #7 publisher worldwide by downloads [E, Sensor Tower via Supercent PR, 5 Dec 2025] [S9]. Play tier 100M+, 4.4 stars, 2.95M reviews, updated 29 Oct 2026 [M] [S10]. Sensor Tower's global 2025 top-10 by downloads lists Pizza Ready #5 [E] [S2]. Revenue is the weak side: AppMagic's Dec 2025 hybrid-casual IAP leaderboard has Supercent at $1.08M for the month (rank 15) versus Rollic $17.5M [E] [S11]; a Gamigion breakdown cites only $774K lifetime IAP at the time (c. 2024) and the game is reported as ~70% ad revenue [E, secondhand] [S12][S13].

**Other reference points.** Idle Miner Tycoon (Kolibri/Ubisoft): Play 100M+, 4.5 stars, updated 28 Oct 2026 [M] [S14]; iOS 4.7 from 866K ratings [M] [S15]. Eatventure (Lessmore, owned by Miniclip since end-2024): Play 50M+, 4.7 stars, 423K reviews [M] [S16]; AppMagic 69.3M downloads and "nearly $28M in IAP" since 2022 [E, Mar 2025] [S17]. Idle Supermarket Tycoon (Codigames): Play 10M+ (India locale shows "1Cr+"), 4.1 stars, ~600K reviews [M] [S18]; iOS 4.6 from 149K ratings [M] [S19]. No lifetime revenue estimate for Idle Supermarket Tycoon was findable.

**India.** 8.45B downloads in FY Apr 2024-Mar 2025 (-8.7% YoY), IAP revenue "just over $400M", simulators are the top download genre at 22% (driving/flight sims lead), 86% male, 77% aged 18-34 [E, Sensor Tower, Jun 2025] [S20][S21]. Ads made up 70.3% of game revenue in India (Jan-May 2026) versus 22.1% in the US [E, Sensor Tower, Jul 2026] [S3]. Pizza Ready! is the #4 most-downloaded game in India in 2025, the only idle-adjacent title in the top 10 [E, AppMagic via Outlook Respawn, 23 Dec 2025] [S22]. On the Google Play India simulation chart snapshot of 16 Feb 2025, the idle titles were Carnival Tycoon (#5) and Idle Lumber Empire (#6, 50M+), and "0% of the top simulation games in India were built by Indian developers" [M, 42matters] [S23]. No Sensor Tower idle-genre list for India was published; those pages 404.

## 2. Current trends

**Idle-arcade (Supercent, Homa, SayGames).** Definition [O, Udonis, 28 May 2025]: a stickman avatar with a joystick collects/stacks, unlocks zones, upgrades avatar/workers/equipment, and idle income continues offline. Top 50 idle-arcade games pull >60M downloads/month and made $6.3M in Oct 2024 combined; the 2024 download ranking was Pizza Ready!, My Perfect Hotel, Outlets Rush, Farm Land, Burger Please! [E, AppMagic via Udonis] [S24]. Retention claims: "almost 50% day-1 retention", 25 min D0 playtime, ~$0.40 CPI [E, Sensor Tower/Homa via Udonis] [S24]. Burger Please monetises with banners, frequent 5-second-skippable interstitials, rewarded video introduced in the tutorial, and a $12.99 bundle (skin, vehicle, no ads, 2x money/speed/capacity) [M, Udonis] [S25]. Supercent says Pizza Ready "delays ad exposure until around three minutes into gameplay" [M, PR] [S9]; players disagree: "there is an ad interruption literally every minute" [R, Play, 6 May 2025] and "For every 60 seconds of game play, there's a 25 second ad" [R, Play, 6 Apr 2025] [S10].

**Hybrid-casual.** Top hybrid-casual titles now beat the top-25 casual games on D7, which fell to 14.9% in Dec 2025 [E, Sensor Tower] [S1][S2]. Deconstructor of Fun calls hybrid-casual "a transitional launch strategy" and notes that once titles scale, "IAP outpaces ad revenue" [O, Jun 2025] [S26]. Hybrid-casual simulators grew 111.5% YoY in revenue [E, AppMagic LiveOps 2025, May 2026] [S27].

**Stage-based idle (Eatventure).** Structure: 60 cities, each a chapter of 7 restaurants; "after completing the last city, the cities will repeat indefinitely" [M, guide, 4 May 2026] [S28]; it starts with a lemonade stand, then food truck, cafe, diner, drive-thru [M, App Store] [S29]. Items (hat/body/hand) give universal bonuses that persist across restaurants; an Invite Helper gives a 5-minute shift every 30 minutes; events rotate weekly; 54.3% of revenue from the US [M/E, AppQuantum on PocketGamer.biz, Oct 2023] [S30]. "Cash Out" as a stage-based idle title: *unknown*; four searches found no such mobile game.

**Merge-tycoon.** Gold & Goblins: Idle Merger is the top-grossing idle game in both US Q1 and Q3 2025 lists [E] [S6][S7]. Merge-2 overall was $1.4B (+80%) in 2025 [E, AppMagic] [S5].

**Local-theme idle wave.** Indonesia: Warung Seblak Nusantara (Gambir Game Studio, May 2024) passed 500K downloads in its first month [M, voi.id] [S31]; Play tier 1M+, IAP Rs 20-280, updated 4 May 2026 [M] [S32], but rated ~2.6 stars [M, Play listing via search] [S32]; Warung Angkringan Nusantara 5M+ [M] [S33]. Turkish doner: Donerci: Kebab Shop Tycoon (dev "ozguro", 100+ installs, updated 15 Sep 2026) and Kebab Shop: Idle Food Tycoon (dev "dahitech", Adana setting, 100+ installs, updated 13 Oct 2026) [M] [S34][S35]. A tapas idle tycoon: *unknown*, none found. [I] The "local" wave is real in Indonesia (millions of installs, local-language), but doner/tapas examples are hobby-scale; nobody has shipped a local-theme idle with first-tier polish.

**Mini-sim / Supermarket-Simulator-likes.** Supermarket Manager Simulator (Digital Melody, Poland) reached #1 weekly global downloads in spring 2024, monetising only with rewarded and interstitials and judged "under-monetized" [O, Lancaric, 17 May 2024] [S36]; Manage Supermarket Simulator (Zego, Vietnam) did 23.2M downloads in June 2024, all Google Play [E, AppMagic via WN Hub, 4 Jul 2024] [S37]; My Supermarket Simulator 3D hit 14.9M Play downloads in Sep 2024 [E, FoxData] [S38]. In 2026 two "Supermarket Simulator" apps sit at 10M+ (3.4 and 4.3 stars) [M] [S39]. [I] This wave is first-person and active, not idle; its relevance to Dhaba Rush is theme appetite (shop-running fantasy), not loop.

## 4. What the leaders shipped in 2025-2026 (table stakes now)

1. **A manager/card layer on top of upgrades.** Idle Supermarket Tycoon added leveling managers in 2026 and board managers for events (Oct 2026) [M] [S19]; Roadside Empire moved managers to level 1 and added 17 more (Aug 2026) [M] [S42]; Idle Miner keeps adding Super Managers [M] [S15]. Players push back when managers gate progress: "they changed it by adding managers you have to level up in order to proceed... Now it's pay-to-win" [R, Play, 23 Aug 2026] [S18]; "you will never have enough bourbon" [R, Play, 13 Dec 2025] [S43].
2. **Weekly events plus an event/season pass, every week.** Eatventure: Potion Shop event (Apr 2025), Farmer's Harvest minigame (May 2025) [M] [S29]; Pizza Ready: Christmas event (Dec 2025), attendance calendar and weekly food contest with skins (Jun 2026) [M] [S29b]; Idle Supermarket: "Longer weekly events" (Oct 2026) [M] [S19]. Industry-wide, average live-ops events per game rose from 73 to 89 per month during 2025; hybrid-casual averaged 64; race events are in 70% of top casual games; 96% ran a holiday event [E, AppMagic, Aug 2025 / May 2026] [S27][S47].
3. **Secondary economies.** Idle Supermarket: Laboratory research, Orders, Gold Bars (Aug 2026) [M] [S19]; Roadside: Fuel Orders, Special Orders, Daily Stories [M] [S42]; Eatventure: pets, stackable boosts [M] [S29].
4. **Serving staff as a mechanic.** Pizza Ready replaced pickup tables with "a service table system where staff deliver food" and upgraded its cleaning robot into a service robot (8 Nov 2025) [M] [S29b]. [I] The market leader has converged on exactly Dhaba Rush's waiter twist, which validates the idea and removes its novelty.
5. **Transparency and QoL.** Eatventure shipped a chest-probability screen and bulk box buying (Jul-Aug 2026) [M] [S29].
6. **Standard IAP ladder.** Every comp sells: remove ads ($3.99-$12.99), permanent x2 income ($5-$10), a pass ($4.99-$10.99), gems/hard currency, and bundled offers. Starter packs appear at $4.99-$6.99 [M] [S10][S15][S29][S42].
7. **Delayed first ad.** Supercent's stated rule is first ad at ~3 minutes; its lower-ad title Snake Clash had 33% of reviews praising minimal ads [M, PR] [S9].

## 5. Where the gaps are

- **Nobody serves India on purpose.** 0% of the top India simulation games are by Indian developers [M, Feb 2025] [S23]; the only Indian-themed idle tycoons are hobby builds (Idle Indian Restaurant Tycoon by KeK Game Factory c. 2022; an Oct 2025 "Indian Restaurant Simulator 3D" APK; chai-tycoon GitHub/itch projects) [M] [S48]. Yet simulators are India's #1 download genre [E] [S21] and Pizza Ready is top-4 [E] [S22].
- **Western price ladders in rupees.** Idle Food Bar tops out at Rs 10,200 and Travel Center Tycoon at Rs 9,100 per item on the India store [M] [S41][S44]; in a market where ads are 70% of revenue [E] [S3], a Rs 299 x2 pack is already mid-ladder.
- **Ad fatigue is the #1 complaint in every comp** [R]: Pizza Ready (every 60 seconds), Roadside Empire ("forced ads when you decline watching an ad", remove-ads "almost $40"), Truck Stop ("Don't expect to get ANYWHERE... without spending money or watching ads"), Idle Food Bar ("at least 75% of them bug out") [S10][S43][S45][S41].
- **Progress walls that look like bugs.** Travel Center's vault level 10 ("There is no way to reach Level 11", reviews May and Sep 2026, "everyone is saying from past 2 years") [R] [S44]; Truck Stop's "money vault limit" [R] [S45].
- **Repetition after the first hour.** "same food, same dishes, every other country, it gets tiring" [R, Eatventure, 4 Sep 2026] [S16]; "Addicting but no progression. Just shop hop" [R, Pizza Ready, Aug 2026] [S29b]; "the fun factor has continuously dropped with each restaurant" [R, Idle Restaurant Tycoon, May 2022] [S40]; "once... the game is just playing itself it isn't worth it" [R, Idle Miner, 5 Sep 2026] [S14].
- **Pop-up overload on open.** "so many pop ups when you first load in... especially for those who paid for no ads" [R, Idle Miner, 6 Sep 2026] [S14]; "too many icons on the screen. I just want to play" [R, Travel Center, 18 Oct 2026] [S44]. [I] This is the same "UI confusing" signal Dhaba Rush's testers gave on 21 Oct; the leaders have it too, and players notice.
- **Dhaba Rush versus table stakes [I].** It has none of items 1-3 in section 4 (no managers, no weekly events, no second location/prestige) and content ends at ~1-1.5 h; every comp above ships multiple locations or a stage loop plus a weekly event cadence.

## 6. Retention and monetisation benchmarks

**All-genre mobile (GameAnalytics).** 2025 report (data through 2024, 11,600 games): D1 top-25% 26.5-27.7% (iOS 31-33%, Android 25-27%), bottom-25% 10-11.5%; D7 median 3.4-3.9%, top-25% 7-8%; 75% of games under 3% D28; median session 5-6 min (top-25% 8-9); ~4 sessions/day; median daily playtime 22 min; Asia sessions ~5 min and playtime 21-22 min [E, Feb 2025] [S49]. 2026 report (16,262 games): D1 median ~22%, top-10% ~40%, top-1% 64-68%; D7 median just under 4%, top-10% 11-12%; D30 median 0.69-0.79%, top-25% 1.6-1.8%; top-25% play 5.3-5.7 sessions/day of ~5.2 min; D1 fell in 2025 versus 2024 [E, Jun 2026] [S50]. Simulation genre averages in the 2025 cut: D7 2.4%, D30 0.5% (casual 3.3% / 0.7%) [E, via Mistplay, Nov 2025] [S51].

**Idle-specific.** GameAnalytics idle study (Dec 2020, the last public idle breakout): stickiness 18% vs 10.5% hyper-casual; 5.3 sessions/day; ~8-min sessions; ARPDAU "nine times" hyper-casual; top-10% D1 45.6%, top-25% 39.4%; top-10% playtime 34.9 min/day [E] [S52]. Appodeal 2025 (US Android, Jun 2024-Jan 2025, >10K games): Idle games serve 73.2 rewarded videos per user (Merge-3 101.5); Management Sim D1 49%; pure hypercasual+simulation grouped at ~3% D30 and $0.79 ARPU [E] [S53]. Arcade-idle: ~50% D1, 25-min D0 playtime, ~$0.40 CPI [E, Sensor Tower/Homa via Udonis, May 2025] [S24]. Kolibri's own Idle Miner numbers: ~10-min sessions, ~8 ads/session, 52% ad engagement, prototype D1 63-81% [M, Feb 2025] [S46].

**Benchmarks for the top end.** Top-25 casual D7 14.9% (Dec 2025); Tasty Travels ~22% D7 [E, Sensor Tower] [S1][S2]. Merge leaders' ARPDAU: Travel Town $0.35, Gossip Harbor $0.26 (Q1 2025) [E] [S54].

**ARPDAU ranges (unattributed vendor compilation, Mar 2026): casual ads-only $0.01-0.05; hypercasual $0.03-0.08; hybrid-casual with 40-60% IAP $0.15-0.50 [O] [S55].** Rewarded video was 17.7% of all ad impressions in 2025 (+53.9% YoY) [E, Sensor Tower] [S1]; Unity's 2024 report puts engagement with the best reward types at ~30% [E, via Mistplay] [S51].

**India.** Ads = 70.3% of game revenue (Jan-May 2026) [E] [S3]. TopOn H1 2025 (secondhand via [S55]): rewarded eCPM in South Asia/India $0.10 versus $8.90 in EU/NA; India Android interstitial eCPM $0.47. India casual CPI ~Rs 78 ($0.89) versus a $1.80 global median [E, AppTweak 2025 via vendor] [S56]. India hyper-casual ARPDAU "~$0.02" and D1 25-35% / D7 5-10% are vendor guesses with no dataset [O] [S56]. No AppsFlyer or Sensor Tower India retention benchmark for idle exists publicly as of Oct 2026.

[I] Implication for the 15 Dec India launch: at $0.10 rewarded eCPM, a player watching 8 rewarded ads/day (the Idle Miner rate) yields under $0.001/day; India revenue will have to come from volume and from the Rs 299 x2 / remove-ads purchases, so D1 above ~40% (idle top-10% territory) and a weekly reason to return matter more than ad density.
