# R6 — Case study: Pizza Ready! (Supercent) and the idle-arcade "feel" school

Researched 31 Oct 2026. Labels used throughout: **[fact]** measured or officially stated, **[est]** third-party estimate (AppMagic / Sensor Tower / consultant), **[opinion]** a named source's view, **[inference]** my own reading. Method note: the shared web-search budget ran out after the first round, so the rest comes from direct fetches; several key pages were blocked (see Gaps).

## 1. What the school is

"Arcade idle" was coined by Homa Games around late 2020 (Farm Land, Craft Island) as the point where hypercasual meets hybrid-casual; the format is "a management game ... about optimization" in which every customer and every machine is a visible timer and the player's avatar is the link between the timers [fact/opinion, Rakib Jahan, 22 Feb 2022]. The canonical loop, as described across the sources: one-finger joystick avatar; products stack physically on the avatar's head/arms up to a capacity cap; cash appears as physical bills on the floor that you walk over to collect; new stations and staff are bought by standing on a pad that drains money from your wallet in a visible stream; hires automate the chore you were doing; then the map expands and the cycle restarts [fact for Burger Please: "the only control is a virtual joystick", stacking, HR office for staff speed/capacity — Udonis, 28 May 2025; fact for My Perfect Hotel: cleaning, restocking, check-in, carrying items, "all ... with one finger", hired cleaners/receptionists then loaders/bartenders/waiters — ARPU Brothers, 16 Apr 2024].

Felix Braberg's summary of Supercent's method: it "reuses proven core mechanics", with "repeated iterations of the 'My Perfect Hotel' formula" re-skinned as Outlets Rush, Pizza Ready and Burger Please [opinion, Gamigion, 11 Apr, year not shown, context 2025]; the 2.5 Gamers podcast: "each new release improving upon the previous one" [opinion, 9 May 2024].

## 2. Pizza Ready! — the numbers

- Developer Molip (Incheon), publisher Supercent (Seoul). Passed **100M downloads within about a year of its 2023 launch** per AppMagic; download split India 21%, Brazil 8%, Indonesia 7%; IAP split US 44%, South Korea 7%, Japan 7%; at the time #3 Simulation on the App Store and #2 free Simulation on Google Play [est, Mobidictum, 13 Jun 2024].
- Later Gamigion profile: **221M downloads**, India 23%, US 7%, Brazil/Indonesia/Russia ~6–7% each; IAP revenue $345,481 in the trailing 30 days; IAP revenue-per-download $0.11 in Tier-1, under $0.05 globally; audience 63% female / 37% male; "No main menu or tutorial; the player starts playing immediately" [est for numbers, fact for the no-menu observation; Gamigion "From Dough to Domination", 8 Apr, year not shown, context 2025]. Supercent's own blog title says "Pizza Ready's Path to 200M Downloads" [fact, Medium, full text not readable].
- Supercent self-reported (search snippets only, pages blocked): CPI "in the $0.20 range" and **D1 retention over 35%** when scaling; the FTUE redesign post reports **+4.5% D1 retention** and **+8 percentage points tutorial retention** [fact as self-reported; absolute D1 after redesign not given].
- Felix Braberg (ad-monetization consultant): Outlets Rush and Pizza Ready are **~70% ads / 30% IAP**, **~8M DAU each "depending on the day"**, **$7–12M/month combined**, user base "holding steady since March 2024" [est, Gamigion, 11 Apr]. The 2.5 Gamers episode put the pair at "close to 9 million per month" [est, 9 May 2024].
- Ad formats: interstitials, rewarded video, banners and app-open ads; Supercent also tested a "public data indexing" partner that gives free premium currency for letting the device be used for web indexing, which Braberg estimated equals 3–4 rewarded impressions per 10 minutes [est/opinion, Gamigion, 11 Apr]. Players noticed: "The click farming this game offers requires you to disable your internet to play." (AdvertHunter, App Store US, 11/04/2024).
- **India today**: Pizza Ready! was the **#4 most-downloaded mobile game in India in Q1 2026** per Sensor Tower, behind Ludo King, Free Fire and Cricket League and ahead of Carrom Pool [est, Tech2, updated 27 Apr 2026]. On 31 Oct 2026 the India App Store shows it **No. 5 in Simulation (Top Free)**, 4.4 stars from 69k ratings, version 60.4.0 dated 15 Oct; No Ads is **Rs 399**, gem pack Rs 299, weekly premium Rs 599, battle pass Rs 499/999, pro pack Rs 1,999 [fact, App Store IN]. US App Store: 4.6 from 347k ratings, #37 Simulation, 388 MB, No Ads $3.99, 10 IAPs from $1.99 to $19.99 [fact, App Store US]. AppBrain reports 4.4 from ~2.73M Google Play ratings [est/snippet]. Update cadence on APKMirror: 10 builds between 1 Apr and 14 Oct 2026, i.e. every 1–6 weeks [fact].
- Market context: India had 8.45B game downloads Apr 2024–Mar 2025; **simulators are the top genre by downloads at 22%**, arcade 19%, puzzle 16%; players are 86% male and 77% aged 18–34 [est, Sensor Tower via GameDev Reports, 30 Jun 2025]. Note the tension with Pizza Ready's 63% female audience [inference: it pulls a different crowd than India's shooter/ludo core; relevant to who Dhaba Rush tests with].

## 3. The siblings

**Burger Please!** (Supercent, 2023). Supercent's own newsroom titles: "#1 in the Global Hyper-Casual Game Genre" (25 Aug 2023) and "ranked 2nd in downloads among all apps released in 2023" (28 Aug 2023) [fact, titles only; pages render as JS]. Udonis (28 May 2025): 20M+ downloads within months [est, AppMagic]; tutorial is step-by-step with **red arrows showing where to go**; **rewarded ads are introduced inside the tutorial**; interstitials appear "relatively frequently" and are **skippable after 5 seconds**; banner at the bottom throughout; the most common rewarded reward is a money bag, with speed/capacity boosts temporary or permanent; premium bundle $12.99 = skin + vehicle + no ads + 2x money/speed/capacity; the author's opinion is that ad cash lets players over-hire early and that a stove upgrade "produced no noticeable change" [opinion] — a feel failure: an upgrade the player cannot see is a wasted purchase.

**Outlets Rush** (Supercent, 2023). ~23M downloads in its first year, mostly India, Pakistan, Indonesia and the US [est, AppMagic via maf.ad; the article now 404s at its Mistplay redirect, so this is from the search snippet]. The same piece argued that interstitials "break the comeback feel the idle rewards are meant to create" [opinion]. UA creatives used real brand logos (Nike, Chanel) later swapped to "Feet Locker" and "Neke" [fact, Gamigion 11 Apr; the 2.5 Gamers show called it a gray area].

**My Perfect Hotel** (Redux.Games / SayGames; Android 13 Apr 2022, iOS 29 Jul 2022). ~133M downloads and $10M+ IAP by mid-2024, "several million dollars per month from ads", 30k+ daily US downloads [est, AppMagic via ARPU Brothers, 16 Apr 2024]; 6M+ downloads and $400k+ revenue in Aug 2024 alone [est, AppMagic via Udonis, 28 Feb 2025]; **~300M downloads** by June 2026 per SayGames' CPO Anton Volnykh, and 700k+ players completed the Barbie event [fact, SayGames blog/GamesBeat, 16 Jun 2026]. SayGames itself had passed 4B downloads [fact, GamesBeat via SayGames blog, 10 Oct 2023]. Volnykh on why they keep iterating: "We want to extend the life cycle of My Perfect Hotel" [fact]. No-ads at $9.99 US / Rs 999 India is "the most popular purchase" [opinion/est, Udonis]. India App Store: 4.6 from 15k, #172 Simulation [fact, 31 Oct 2026]. US: 4.7 from 710k, #46 Simulation [fact].

### The measured first session of My Perfect Hotel (iOS) — ARPU Brothers, 16 Apr 2024 [fact]
- No tutorial. Start with $50 at reception. First room costs $30; cleaning a room takes ~6 s.
- 1:46 cleaner unlocks. 2:19 first rewarded ad, pays $100 (Android pays $150). 4:20 cleaner hired. ~5:00 second ad, $100. A $150 rewarded ad auto-appears when the player is $64 short.
- 9:26 automatic receptionist unlocks after the third ad. 11:29 hotel level 1 complete. Session ends ~12 min with 5 ads watched, 3 rooms, a toilet, a utility room, 2 automated staff.
- Task progress is pinned on the left ("4 out of 30 tasks"); stars for tasks done personally are needed to level the hotel. Quests only after hotel level 3; daily rewards at level 4.
- Over 19 sessions the author watched **528 ads** (session counts 5, 17, 40, 38, 23, 14, 16, 28, 15, 15, 46, 32, 23, 14, 27, 73, 29, 32, 41); ad length ranged 6 s to 7 min 43 s. Opinion: "I believe the developers at Say Games have balanced player progression and monetization very well" and "The UI is very well designed to not overwhelm the player".

Martin Jurasek's 15-minute read (11 Oct 2023) [opinion]: "Straight from the get-go, the game gives you a lot of simple tasks to complete"; every collected cash stack "feels rewarding"; after a couple of minutes upkeep becomes a bottleneck, which is exactly when hiring is offered; "I just hired receptionist as a first staff, but I still have to bring the toilet paper"; "In my first 15 minutes, there were no daily quests and not even a hard currency implemented!" His five lessons: rewarding micro-tasks with strong feedback; automate the chores but **keep money collection manual** because that is the satisfying part; rewarded ads whose reward changes how you play (unicycle / wooden horse / flying carpet skins, not a flat +50%); the VIP guest who drops a trail of dollars you chase; introduce mechanics gradually.

## 4. Why it dominates downloads

[fact] Zero-friction start (no menu, no tutorial text; arrows and pads teach by doing), a universally legible theme (pizza/burger/hotel), tiny CPIs (~$0.20 self-reported) and a 70/30 ad-led model that makes India-scale free installs profitable even at <$0.05 IAP per download. [inference] The joystick-plus-stacks grammar is also the perfect UA creative: 10 seconds of gameplay video explains the whole game, which Udonis notes SayGames' ads do with "basic gameplay ads showing core mechanics". [inference] In India specifically, simulators are already the biggest download genre and Pizza Ready's chart position (#4 overall in Q1 2026, #5 Simulation on iOS today) is the most relevant single data point for Dhaba Rush: the Indian Android audience already downloads this exact loop at scale; the open question is whether a hands-off tap-tycoon can deliver the same feel.

Praise, usually in the same breath as the complaint:
- My Perfect Hotel, App Store US: Totally_A_Person (20 Jan 2024) calls it addictive and praises avatars and skins; ForeverBroken (20 Nov 2023) "loves the game" but wants the 3-minute vehicle boost to be 5 minutes and notes lag when collecting coins; Kueen Cargill (12 Sep 2024) reports guests idling after service and "cash stacks placed where characters can't collect them".
- My Perfect Hotel, App Store India: Anaisha and Avyaan (14 Nov 2024) rate it "outstanding" while calling ads too frequent.
- [inference] Stores surface critical reviews, so praise is thin here; the 4.4–4.7 averages across millions of ratings are the real praise. Named when praising: addictive, avatar/skins, the early hours. Named when quitting: ad cadence, a content ceiling ("level 11"), and feel bugs (lag on coin pickup, unreachable cash stacks).

## 7. Anatomy of the feel [inference, synthesised from the above]

1. **Everything is physical.** Money is paper on the floor, products are a stack on your head, prices are a pad that visibly drains. Nothing important lives only in a HUD number.
2. **The player is the bottleneck, briefly.** You do the chore 3–10 times, it becomes annoying at minute 2–3, and the hire appears exactly then (MPH: cleaner unlock 1:46, hired 4:20).
3. **A new thing every 1–3 minutes for the first 12 minutes** (MPH timeline), then widening gaps filled by rewarded ads.
4. **Keep the pleasure, automate the pain**: staff take chores, the player keeps collecting cash (Jurasek).
5. **Rewards that change the verb**, not just the number (unicycle skin, VIP cash trail).
6. **Instant start**: no menu, no text; arrows and pads.
7. **Feedback density**: every pickup, drop, pay and hire has a sound, a pop and a particle; the feel economy is "many small confirmations per second".

## 8. What transfers to a hands-off tap-tycoon without changing the control scheme — 12 techniques

Dhaba Rush keeps tap-to-open-sheet. These port the *feel*, not the joystick.

1. **Drain-pad spending (feedback on spend).** Pizza Ready / MPH: standing on a pad streams bills out of your wallet with a ticking counter and the station builds up in stages. Dhaba: on LEVEL UP / BUILD, fly a burst of rupee notes from the galla to the counter, tick the price down over ~0.6 s with a per-chunk "thunk", and let the counter visibly upgrade (new tandoor plate, bigger kadai) as the last note lands. Burger Please's unreadable stove upgrade is the anti-pattern.
2. **Purchase cadence tuned to the MPH clock.** Something new affordable at ~1:45, ~4:20, ~9:30, ~11:30 in session one (ARPU Brothers). Dhaba: price the first chai upgrade, first cook, first extra charpai and the tandoor so the NEXT chip flips green on roughly that schedule; use Pecorella's ~1.07 per-level growth for the long tail.
3. **Physical cash at the galla.** All four games pile bills on the floor; MPH players complain when stacks are unreachable, i.e. collection is the pleasure. Dhaba: let paid bills stack as note bundles on the galla counter, growing in height; a tap sweeps them with a rising-pitch coin tick per bundle (Jurasek lesson 2: keep collection manual even after cashiers are hired, or make the cashier's auto-sweep a visible, hirable upgrade).
4. **Visible stacks at the pass.** Finished dishes should stack as plates on the pass and ride visibly on the waiter's tray; a queue of 6 plates is a bottleneck the player can read without a number. Dhaba already has waiters; make the stack the UI.
5. **Red-arrow guidance instead of tutorial text.** Burger Please uses red arrows through the whole tutorial. Dhaba: a pulsing arrow from the galla to the first affordable counter, and nothing else on screen for the first 60 s.
6. **Instant start.** Gamigion on Pizza Ready: no main menu, no tutorial; MPH: start with $50 and a $30 room. Dhaba: boot into the dhaba with a truck already braking and a traveller walking in within 5 s; first chai sale within 15 s; first affordable upgrade within 45 s.
7. **Do-it-yourself then automate.** MPH hires one role at a time and leaves a chore behind ("still have to bring the toilet paper"). Dhaba: before the first waiter, let the player tap a ready plate to send it to a table (one tap, no joystick); after 5–8 taps the WAITER button appears. The hire then feels like relief, not a menu item.
8. **Pinned micro-task counter.** MPH's "4 out of 30 tasks" on the left edge plus stars for tasks done personally. Dhaba's tasks bar + chest should show "3/5" at all times, with the chest shaking when one task is left.
9. **Rewarded ads that change the verb.** MPH's unicycle/flying-carpet skins and the VIP guest dropping a dollar trail. Dhaba: the ad bubble can spawn a "Bada Saab" in a white Scorpio who tips a trail of notes across the courtyard, or put the waiter on a cycle for 3 minutes (MPH players asked for 5). This is also a honest alternative to a bare x2.
10. **Scaled cash chest.** MPH's suitcase scales with progress ($100 at 2:19). Dhaba: show the rewarded reward as a cloth money bag with the amount expressed as "= 6 min of income", so it stays worth it at every level.
11. **Sound and number pops on every event.** Juice-it-or-lose-it: layered feedback for minimal input; Swink: rank the cues. Dhaba: a distinct pitch per event (chai pour, tandoor slap, plate on tray, note on galla, hire stamp), floating "+Rs 12" pops that scale with amount, and the galla total tweening (ease-out) rather than snapping. Juul & Begy's caveat: juice lifts ratings but not performance, so keep the critical info (timers, NEXT chip) un-juiced and readable.
12. **Camera as reward.** Idle-arcade games pull the camera back and pan to each new area on unlock. Dhaba: on BUILD, pan to the new counter, hold 1 s, pulse the hire button, then return; on each 10 customers served, a tiny 2–3 px screen nudge on the galla sweep (Vlambeer: shake scaled to impact, never constant).

Two guardrails from the reviews: no interstitial within 60 s of a purchase, hire or rewarded reward (maf.ad: interstitials "break the comeback feel"; Burger Please makes them skippable after 5 s), and plan the content ceiling before launch, since "maxed out level 11" is the second most common quit reason.
