# Timed teardown, minute 3 to day 3: IST v4, Eatventure, Idle Restaurant Tycoon, Idle Miner Tycoon

Researched 1 Nov 2026. Labels: [M] measured or official, [E] estimate, [R] review, [O] opinion, [I] my inference.

**Method.** No 2025-2026 written teardown of these games turned up (ARPU Brothers, Lancaric, GameRefinery, Udonis, AppAgent searched). I measured beats from the YouTube storyboards (thumbnail strips sampled every 5-10 s) of 2025-2026 "part 1" videos. Times are **video time**; uploaders may cut footage or ads, so an ad's absence is not proof. Large headings are legible; prices and body text mostly are not, and are marked illegible rather than guessed.

**Opening checklist versus 2019-2022** (Gamezebo 2020; LevelWinner 2019):
- **Still there [M]:** the produce opener ("Overripe Fruit"), the assistant character, and a task bar.
- **2x ad button:** the classic play-icon 2x button could not be identified in the frames. In v4 the 2x now sits under Management > Boosters, next to an ad-watch "VIP Campaign" track [M].
- **Task bar and chest:** in v3.2.6 the MISSIONS panel shows "TASKS" with a chest icon and a "Completed x/10" counter (Ru, 1:45). The v4 top bar shows three task cards, and I could not see a chest in it [M].
- **The VIP / x2 popup at about 2.5 minutes is a v4 beat [I].** It was not seen in 12 minutes of the Feb 2025 v3.2.6 run.
- **Changed: the first location.** It is now a two-department "Supply Town" that ends with a move to Suburban Market. This was already true in v3.2.6, where the move came at 11:40-11:55 (Ru). It replaced the Miami Beach and Beverly Hills cities, which gave x50 multipliers (LevelWinner 2019).
- **Changed: the department panel.** It went from rows (Upgrade / Hire Employee / Greengrocer's Salary) in v3.2.6 to three buttons plus a manager card in v4 [M].
- **When Supply Town arrived:** the iOS notes do not date it. My guess is version 3.0.0 or 3.1.0 (Oct-Nov 2023: "visual rework", "new supermarket") [I].

**Interstitials.** None appear in about 55 minutes of recorded 2026 play (S, T1, T2) [M, but cuts are possible]. Reviews tell a different story:
- April 2024 (v3.2.3-3.2.5): several reviews describe a forced ad every 45-60 seconds, with an on-screen countdown, arriving after the introductory offers [R].
- 16 Mar 2026 (v4.0.2, GB, 3★): a new player says ads came one after another from the very start [R].
- 17 Jun 2026 (Google Play, 4★, v4.1.5): card packs open only every 5 minutes, and each needs a 45-second ad [R].

**Session end and return.** I saw no return screen in any video. The official 2019-era rules were 2 hours of offline earnings by default, 10 hours with the General Manager purchase (still $9.99 on the App Store in Nov 2026), and an ad to double or 20 gems to triple on return [M, older]. A 22 Apr 2026 review (v4.0.2) says only about 10 minutes of earnings were credited after a 10-hour absence [R]. Several reviews in 2026 say free manager packs refresh every 24 hours, which acts as a day-2 hook [R]. No notification texts are documented anywhere.

**Days 1-3 [R].**
- One player went through all the stores in a day and then had to watch ads in the last one (GB, 20 Jul 2026, v4.1.6).
- Another finished all levels in a couple of days (IN, 18 Sep 2026).
- Another beat the whole game in under about 30 hours (US, 19 Feb 2026).
- After the manager update, others say a single section can take days (US, 19 Sep 2026) or that they were stuck for over a week waiting for cards (US, 25 May 2026).
- One review says moving stores means starting over with zero progress (US, 30 Jul 2026).
- What carries over: v4's multiplier and carry-over rules are not documented. Manager cards appear to be a global collection [I].
- **Length of the first location: about 0.15-0.4 hours** (9 to 25 minutes) [M].

**Structure [M, wiki].** City 1, San Francisco, has seven outlets: Lemonade Stand (1 station, max level 25), Food Stand (2, 50), Food Truck (3, 75), Cafe (4, 150), Fast Food (5, 150), Drive Thru (7, 150) and Restaurant (10, 250). Renovating needs every station maxed. The flight to the next city keeps the **Vault** (12 permanent cards bought with gems; the Tip Jar is free), gear from boxes (40 or 200 gems) and pets. Investors appear at random for 15 seconds and give cash for an ad. Clubs open at city 7. Store IAP prices are $0.99 to $26.99, starting with a Daily Offer and a Permanent Boost at $5.39 [M].

**Day scale [R].**
- A new player who played most of the day was about to finish San Francisco after roughly 4 days (Google Play IN, 11 Apr 2026, 5★).
- Another found the first stage took days, with prices climbing fast within a few levels (GB, 15 Dec 2024).
- Another spent 3 days on one New York outlet (GB, 14 Feb 2026).
- The game has a "Speed Runner" achievement for finishing a city in 30 minutes, which is an endgame pace.
- Offline earnings cap at 3 hours [R, 12 Jul 2026; matches the package].
- In June 2026 (v1.51) one player was forced to watch an ad to claim offline earnings (7 Jun 2026). Version 1.53.2 (11 Aug 2026) "fix[ed] the ad button on the offline earnings popup" [M].
- "No forced ads" is the most common praise from 2022 to Oct 2026 [R].
- **Length of the first location: days, not hours.**

## 6. Inference [I]

**Minimum day-scale structure for a hands-off tycoon at soft launch (hour 1 to day 3):**
1. **Show the next location from about minute 3** as a priced, locked card, the way IST lists Commercial City and Eatventure shows the Before/After renovation preview.
2. **A first move at 10-25 minutes.** IST now ends its first location in that window and turns the reset into a tutorial beat. Dhaba Rush's first content lasts 35-50 minutes with no move, so its first "promotion" comes later than the market leader's.
3. **A second location with 3 named sub-goals** (IST's Bakery, Fresh Market, Frozen Site) that fill hours 1-3, and a **third location priced to land on day 1-3**.
4. **A capped return**: 2-3 hours by default (IST and Eatventure; IRT's 1 hour is a top complaint), an ad to double the earnings, and something that refreshes every 24 hours (IST's free pack) to bring players back for session 2.
5. **An event or season surface by day 2-3.** IST reaches one about 17 minutes after the move. IRT shows a season pass at 9 minutes.
6. **No interstitials in the first sessions.** Eatventure's four years of "no forced ads" praise contrasts with the 1-star reviews of IST's April 2024 ad wave.
7. **Avoid a paid gate in location 2.** IST's manager gate at about 26 minutes is the most-hated beat in its 2026 reviews.

**IST v4 opening beats that differ from 2019-2022:** a two-department tutorial town ending in a guided move at 9-25 minutes (replacing a multi-hour first city with a x50 multiplier); the three-button panel with a manager card; a server-backed VIP popup at about 2.5 minutes; a quest bar since about Feb 2026; Boosters, VIP Campaign and Inventory; free manager packs, research tokens and an event hop in location 2. Dhaba Rush's "x2 VIP popup plus free reward" matches **v4**, not 2019-2022 [I].
