# Dhaba Rush: the core and the feel

Research report, 1 Nov 2026, on version 0.3.7. It answers four questions: what the core of this kind of game is, what its players look for, what they get today, and what the leading games teach. Section 6 turns the answers into a plan for Dhaba Rush.

**How it was made.** Seven research sweeps on the web, each fact-checked by a second agent, then three more sweeps on gaps a critic found: timed openings of today's leaders, games with seated service and waiters, and Indian players' own reviews. The notes are in [research/](research/), and section 8 lists them. Section 7 lists the claims that failed fact-checking. Nothing in the game was changed.

Market figures from Sensor Tower or AppMagic are third-party estimates. Player reviews are paraphrased here; the notes keep the originals with dates.

---

## 1. The answer on one page

**The core.** A hands-off idle tycoon is a small machine the player watches and tunes. Customers flow through stations, money flows in, and the player's one real decision is which part of the machine to fix next. Four things surround that decision, and the genre cannot do without them: something affordable every minute or two early on, a place that visibly grows, money waiting when you come back, and a bigger next place once the current one is done. Everything else is decoration. (r1, r4, r5)

**What players look for.** Calm. Ads they choose, which always pay. One obvious next thing. A place to watch grow, with charm. Fair progress with no paywall. A reason to come back tomorrow. And, for the minority who optimise, a bottleneck to reason about. (r2, g3)

**What they get today.** Polished games whose first hour is good and whose shell fights the player: forced ads, pop-ups on launch, paid manager cards, walls that look like bugs, and the same restaurant again after the first hour. The leaders keep adding managers, weekly events and new locations. Nobody builds for India on purpose, and no dhaba idle game exists. (r3, r7, g3)

**What the case studies teach.**
- **Idle Supermarket Tycoon** (the reference): the opening Dhaba Rush copied is the part its players still call fun. What kept it alive for seven years came after the opening: new cities with income multipliers, permanent upgrades, events. Its 2026 rebuild put paid manager cards in front of upgrades, and its reviews collapsed. (r4, g2)
- **Eatventure**: the best-rated food idle game wins on trust (no forced ads), on play that needs no text, and on small arcs you finish and leave. (r5)
- **Idle Restaurant Tycoon**: the closest game to Dhaba Rush's seats-and-waiters twist. It made the bottleneck readable with a door counter. It lost players when guests walked out and the player had no way to prevent it, and when new content stopped. (r5, g1)
- **Pizza Ready and My Perfect Hotel**: the feel school. Everything is physical, something new arrives every one to three minutes, and the player does a chore briefly before hiring someone for it. Pizza Ready switched to staff carrying food to tables in Nov 2025. (r6, g1)
- **India's local hits** (Truck Masters, Indian Bikes Driving 3D, Bus Simulator India): players reward recognition, horns, real vehicles, their own state, offline play and small downloads. They punish ads that block play. Truck and bus sim players ask, unprompted, for a dhaba stop. (r7)

**What Dhaba Rush must nail.** The first three minutes already work, the twist is the right core, and the market is moving toward it. Three things stand between the game and the genre's core:
1. **There is no day loop.** New things stop at 35 to 50 minutes and goals at about 1 to 1.5 hours. Every leader hands the player a new place or a renovation by then. Without one, the soft-launch day-3 and day-7 numbers will measure the content wall, not the game.
2. **The twist doesn't yet read in the world.** Travellers walk out after 8 seconds even when the player could not have helped, which every seated-service game's reviews punish. Half of new players never get the seats lesson. Hires from the STAFF panel happen behind it, so the purchase that matters most is the one players see least.
3. **The ad plan breaks the calm.** Interstitials after purchases from session 4 match the exact pattern players describe when they uninstall, and India's ad rates make them worth little.

**One precondition comes before all three: the game has never run on a phone.** For Indian players, a game that won't start, loads slowly or eats data is the top reason for a 1-star review (g3). Every feel judgement in this report is unverified until it has.

**The smallest day loop is a renovation, not a new map.** All three syntheses and the case studies point the same way: a priced, locked "Family Dhaba" card shown from about minute 3, taken when the current content runs out, that keeps the player's place, adds an AC family hall and multiplies income (section 6.4).

## 2. The core of the genre

### 2.1 The loop at four time scales

| Scale | What happens | Dhaba Rush today |
|---|---|---|
| Seconds | Agents walk, wait on a timer, pay. Money pops. The player taps a station and buys. | Present and polished |
| Minutes | Costs race income. Something is always affordable. A red link in the chain says what to fix. | Present: the NEXT and PROBLEM chip |
| Session, 3 to 15 min | Collect what built up, spend it down, claim a task, bank one milestone, leave when the next thing is minutes away. | Present for about the first hour |
| Day and week | Capped offline money brings you back. A new place or a renovation multiplies income. A weekly event gives a reason to return. | Missing |

Sources: r1 (Anthony Pecorella's idle-game maths; guides to Idle Supermarket Tycoon, Idle Miner Tycoon and Eatventure), g2.

### 2.2 The six parts that are the core

1. **The seesaw.** Costs rise faster than income, and multipliers at milestones pull income back ahead. Tuned well, something is always affordable in the first ten minutes, and a visible next goal arrives every three to five minutes. (r1)
2. **A bottleneck the player can see and fix.** Miner, elevator and warehouse in Idle Miner Tycoon. Department, checkout and parking in Idle Supermarket Tycoon. The chain is the game's only real decision. In Kolibri's playtests, players who never noticed the bottleneck quit, and on-screen prompts fixed it. (r1, r5)
3. **The place visibly grows.** Players praise upgrades that change the scene, and they call an upgrade that changes nothing visible a waste. (r2, r6)
4. **Doing becomes delegating.** Managers in Idle Miner Tycoon, staff in Pizza Ready. The fantasy is watching the business run itself. (r1)
5. **The day loop.** Capped offline earnings, a reason to return, and a reset or new location that multiplies income. When the content ends, players leave: Kolibri's first Idle Miner build lost players as soon as they finished it. (r1, r5)
6. **Calm.** No fail state, no timers, ads by choice, at most one pop-up on launch. This year's loudest complaints across every leader are pop-ups and ad density. (r1, r2)

### 2.3 What is decoration

The theme, the vehicles, day and night, music, the guide character, the dish names, the chest animation and the VIP pop-up styling. Decoration sells the game and makes it loved, but it cannot rescue a broken seesaw or an unreadable chain. For Dhaba Rush the theme matters more than usual, but mostly for installs. Recognition dominates the 5-star reviews of Indian driving sims (section 5.5), yet in Indian reviews of idle games, India or Hindi themes come up in only 0.3% of reviews in either star band (g3). **The theme gets the install; the core earns the rating.**

A test from r1, worth using on every tester: **at minute 8, can they say what is slowing the dhaba down, what they will buy next, and what the dhaba will look like after?** If yes, the core is there.

## 3. What players look for

Ranked from more than 30,000 store reviews across about fifteen games, Reddit threads and studio data (r2, g1, g3).

| Rank | What they want | What they say, in short |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ads they choose, which always pay | Eatventure has been praised every year since 2022 for having no forced ads. A rewarded ad that pays nothing, crashes or reloads the game earns a 1-star review. |
| 2 | Any session length, with money waiting on return | Play five minutes or an hour without losing ground. Offline money feels like a gift. |
| 3 | A calm screen with one obvious next thing | Players count the pop-ups on launch and uninstall over icon clutter. |
| 4 | A place to watch grow, with charm | Watching the place grow is the most common 5-star theme, along with humour in upgrade names. |
| 5 | Fair progress, no paywall | Manager cards and research tokens that gate upgrades are this year's top 1-star cause in Codigames' games. |
| 6 | Upgrades that change what they see | An upgrade with no visible change reads as wasted money. |
| 7 | Always a next place or event | "Nothing else to do" and "I finished it, please update" end the game for many. |
| 8 | Something to think about | Optimisers want honest per-minute numbers and a bottleneck to solve, and call games without decisions shallow. |
| 9 | Their progress is never lost | One lost save or one reset update spends years of trust. |
| 10 | Honesty | The game should match its store ad, and updates should never take back what was earned. |

**Two kinds of player want opposite screens.** Cosy players write most of the 5-star text. They want one next action, a quiet screen and life in the scene. Optimisers are loud in 2- and 3-star text. They want honest numbers, the bottleneck named, and cost against payoff for each upgrade. A screen can serve both by showing one next thing up front and the numbers one tap away. (r2)

**Why players quit** (r2, g2, g3):

| When | Why |
|---|---|
| First session | Ads before trust. A wait in the first minutes. One confusing step. The game won't load on a cheap phone. |
| Day 1 | The loop shrinks to open, spend, close. The second place looks like the first. |
| Week 1 | Interstitials switch on "after a few days". The content runs out. A hard gate. A lost save. |

The evidence splits on the top spot. Across the whole India-store sample, ads separate bad reviews from good ones most sharply. In the Hindi-locale reviews, "won't start or loads slowly" comes first. Both lead to the same fixes. (g3)

**Indian players, in their own words** (r7, g3). Hindi-locale reviews are the reliable Indian voice, because English reviews pulled from the Indian store are mostly foreign. Their top complaints in 1- to 3-star reviews: the game won't start or loads slowly, it eats data and MB, it doesn't match its ad, and the ads are too many or too long. Their praise: time-pass, fun, and playing without internet. The most-helpful review in the whole Indian sample praises Pizza Ready for working offline. Requests for Hindi are rare, and most Hindi-locale reviewers write in Roman script. One Hindi reviewer of an idle game said they understood nothing because everything ran by itself. That is the testers' "hard to understand" in another form: without a stated job, "it plays itself" reads as confusion.

## 4. What players get today

### 4.1 The market, Oct 2026

| Game | Type | Google Play | How it extends play | Ads |
|---|---|---|---|---|

### 4.2 Table stakes in late 2026

What the leaders shipped in 2025 and 2026 (r3, g1, g2):
- A manager or card layer on top of upgrades. Players accept it when it is earned in play and revolt when it is a paid gate.
- Weekly events, usually with a pass.
- Side systems: research, orders, pets, achievements.
- Staff who carry food to tables (Pizza Ready, Nov 2025).
- No ad before about three minutes of play (Supercent's stated rule).
- The same price ladder everywhere: Remove Ads, permanent ×2 income, a pass, a starter pack.

### 4.3 The gaps

- **Ad fatigue is the top complaint in every comparable game.** Ads come up in nearly half of the 1- to 3-star reviews in the India-store sample (g3).
- **Pop-ups on launch.** The same "UI confusing" signal Dhaba Rush's testers gave.
- **Walls that look like bugs**, such as Travel Center Tycoon's vault stuck at level 10 for two years.
- **Repetition after the first hour**: the same food in every city.
- **Western price ladders in rupees**, above ₹10,000 per item, in a market where ads are 70% of game revenue.
- **Nobody builds for India.** No Indian studio is in the top simulation charts. The India-themed idle games are tiny: Idle Indian Restaurant has 10K+ installs, Chaiwala Tycoon 5K+. The closest rival in mechanic, Rest Stop Tycoon, is a US-skinned highway stop with 100K+ installs. No dhaba idle game exists. (r3, r7)

**The honest read on India: this is white space, not proven demand.** Indians download the idle-tycoon loop at scale in foreign skins (Pizza Ready was a top-5 download in India in 2025), and Hindi reviewers read it as a small-business fantasy. No local-theme idle game has yet shown that the theme lifts it. (r7, g3)

## 5. Case studies

### 5.1 Idle Supermarket Tycoon: the game Dhaba Rush copied

(r4, g2)

**What it is.** Codigames' 2019 hit, the most-downloaded strategy game in the US in May 2019 by the company's account. On it and three sister games, revenue went from €1.6M in 2018 to €71.8M in 2020. It then fell to about €20M by 2024, and the studio cut a fifth of its staff.

**Seven things that make it work.**
1. The machine is always visible, and you cannot lose money.
2. One sheet with three levers per department, and the next affordable thing is always obvious.
3. A stats screen flags the bottleneck in red.
4. Upgrades change how departments look.
5. Optional ad boosts double income rather than gate progress.
6. A long horizon from day one: the next city's price and multiplier.
7. City moves multiply income and keep unlocked building types, so a reset feels like a promotion. That is the rule for versions 1 to 3; version 4's carry-over is undocumented.

**What its players wish it did better.** Progress slows sharply after the tutorial. Prices jump between cities. Ads creep up and sometimes fail to pay. Timers were bolted onto instant actions. Paid manager cards arrived in 2026. Variety is thin.

**The 2026 rebuild matters for Dhaba Rush.** The 162-second video Dhaba Rush copied matches the current version 4: the task bar and the VIP ×2 pop-up at about 2:30 are version-4 beats. In that version, the copied opening is the first ten minutes of a small two-department town that ends in a guided move to a second supermarket, somewhere between 9 and 25 minutes in. About 26 minutes into play, paid manager cards start gating upgrades. In the Indian-store sample, English reviews since version 4 average below 2 stars. **Dhaba Rush copied the opening but not the promotion that follows it, and should never copy the card gate.**

**Verdict on the copied three minutes: keep them.** The hands-off loop with visible money, the three-lever sheet, the bottleneck readout, a first new counter inside the session, and the rewarded ad framed as a gift are the core. The guide character, the specific departments and the VIP styling are incidental. The warning from its reviews: the part that stays fun is the part Dhaba Rush copied. What bought Codigames seven more years came after it.

### 5.2 Eatventure: why it out-rates the reference

(r5, g2)

Lessmore, a German studio of three founders who made the art themselves, launched it in 2022. Miniclip bought the studio at the end of 2024, and it is now a team of about 33. By early 2025 it had about 69M downloads and $28M in purchases (AppMagic estimates). It rates 4.7★ on Play against Idle Supermarket Tycoon's 4.1★.

Why players rate it higher:
1. **The ad contract.** No forced ads, praised every year since 2022. The rewarded ad is a character: suited "investors" walk in among the customers.
2. **Small arcs you finish.** Each restaurant is renovated in place, from lemonade stand to food stand to food truck to café, and then the city is sold and you move on. A preview of the renovation appears at 3:00 and the first one lands at about 5:30. The typical complaint shifts from "nothing to do" (1 and 2 stars) to "repetitive" (3 and 4 stars).
3. **No text needed.** The studio's stated aim is visuals that tell the story without text. There is no guide character.
4. **A permanent layer that isn't sold as a gate**: gear, a vault of permanent perks, pets, clubs.
5. **A fixed weekly calendar**: a weekend event in a bigger restaurant and a mid-week race. A two-person team can copy the shape of that cycle, not its weekly pace.

Its weak spots are long dead stretches mid-city and the same dishes in every country. A dhaba game has a natural answer to the second: each highway stop gets its own regional menu.

### 5.3 Idle Restaurant Tycoon: the closest game to the twist

(r5, g1)

Kolibri built it in six months in 2020 as a deliberate move from Idle Miner Tycoon's three-link chain to linked bottlenecks. Hosts seat guests, cooks set speed, each waiter serves two tables, cleaners clear tables and parking brings guests. A door counter shows vacant tables, and an overview tab splits profit by area.

What its soft launch taught: many players got stuck at one early tutorial step, and most missed an ad placement that wasn't highlighted. Both were reworked and the numbers rose.

What its players punished:
- Guests walking out when a table frees a second later, or when the extra guests an ad brings find no seat. In hands-off games, walkouts are hated whenever the player had no way to prevent them. Walkouts are only praised in games where the player is the server (g1).
- Waiters who cannot be made faster.
- Bad pathing: waiters walking back to their station between tables, and a big table that never gets cleaned.
- Content that stopped: the same weekly events for months, then only bug fixes.

Players liked watching the staff scurry and the absence of pressure.

### 5.4 Pizza Ready and My Perfect Hotel: the feel school

(r6, g1)

Pizza Ready was the most-downloaded hybrid-casual game of 2025, with about 150M installs that year (Sensor Tower, via Supercent). India is its biggest market by downloads. About 70% of its revenue comes from ads.

A measured first session of My Perfect Hotel, SayGames' idle-arcade benchmark (ARPU Brothers, 2024):

| Time | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0:00 | No tutorial. $50 at reception; the first room costs $30. |
| 1:46 | A cleaner unlocks |
| 2:19 | First rewarded ad, framed as a bag of cash |
| 4:20 | Cleaner hired |
| 9:26 | Automatic receptionist |
| 11:29 | Hotel level 1 complete |

What makes the feel:
1. **Everything is physical.** Money is paper on the floor, food is a stack, and prices are a pad that drains.
2. **The player is the bottleneck, briefly.** A chore gets annoying around minute two or three, and the hire appears just then.
3. **Something new every one to three minutes** for the first twelve minutes.
4. **Automate the pain, keep the pleasure.** Staff take the chores; the player keeps collecting the cash.
5. **Rewards that change how you play**, such as a skin or a guest who drops a trail of money, not only a bigger number.
6. **An instant start**, with arrows instead of text.
7. **Many small confirmations every second**: a sound, a pop and a particle for every pickup, drop, payment and hire.

What its players hate: ads (Hindi reviewers describe ads every few seconds) and the content ceiling, where players max out the last shop and find nothing after it.

**The waiter change.** In Nov 2025 Pizza Ready replaced self-service pickup with a service table where staff deliver the food. A scan of 5,401 reviews found no praise and no backlash: players noticed ads, not who carried the food. That validates Dhaba Rush's twist as natural. It also means the twist alone is not a selling point. How well it reads and feels is.

### 5.5 India's local-theme hits

(r7, g3)

| Game | Installs | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Indian Bikes Driving 3D | 100M+ | 169 MB; praised as offline and light on storage |
| Bus Simulator Indonesia / Bus Simulator India (Maleo) | 100M+ / 10M+ | "Iconic Indian horn sounds"; players ask for their own state |
| Truck Masters: India (Highbrow) | 10M+ | 598 MB; sells desi pressure horns; ads gate jobs |
| Masala Express, Indian Cooking Star | 10M+ each | Indian cooking, time-management |

What Indian players reward:
- **Recognition.** Words like real, Indian and desi appear in 75% of Bus Simulator India's 4- and 5-star reviews, against 43% of its 1- to 3-star ones.
- **Horns** as a signature sound, real vehicles, and "add my state".
- **Pride.** Players address the Indian developer by name.
- **The business fantasy.** Hindi reviewers recommend Pizza Ready to anyone who runs a business.
- **A dhaba stop.** Truck and bus sim players ask for one without being prompted. Drivers call a dhaba a "hotel".

What they punish: ads that block the core loop; a Remove Ads purchase that still shows ads; "offline" games that need data; heat and lag on cheap phones; shallow or wrong authenticity, such as the wrong region's dish, identical towns or empty streets; and store ads that don't match the game.

The recognition figures come from driving sims, not idle games, so treat them as a guide to what earns installs.

**Dhaba Rush is well placed on the practical side, but that is expected, not a selling point.** Its release download is about 34 MB, and only ads and analytics need a connection. Size is hygiene: hits of 169 to 929 MB still reach 10M+ when players want them. Offline play, though, is praised by name. The authenticity checklist in r7 section 6 lists the details Indian players will notice.

## 6. Dhaba Rush: the core and the feel to nail

### 6.1 The core promise

> You own a busy Indian highway dhaba. Lorries pull in, travellers sit on charpais, waiters run trays and the galla keeps ringing. Your job is to spot what is holding the line up, fix it with a tap, and grow a chai stall into the most famous stop on the highway.

**The feel in one line: busy but never stressful, and every rupee you spend shows up in the dhaba.**

### 6.2 Where it stands

What already works (p0):
- **The first three minutes are tight and measured.** A sale at 0:37, a hire at about 1:10, and both twist lessons opened by a visible problem and closed in two taps.
- **The NEXT and PROBLEM chip** turns a confusing simulation into one sentence and one tap.
- **The numbers are honest.** "/min" is real takings, and every hire shows before and after.
- **The world reads as a dhaba**: whole orders on trays, lorries reversing into bays, fairy lights, harmonium and tabla, four Indian horns.
- **Purchase juice is in** and costs almost nothing on low-end settings.
- **The in-world hire card** for a waiter or a table already buys in two taps and pops the purchase in where the player is looking.
- **The build is small and works offline.**

What breaks the core or the feel (p0, checked against the research and the code):

| Problem | Why it matters | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| No day loop: new things stop at 35 to 50 min, goals at about 1 to 1.5 h | This is exactly where every leader hands the player a new place, and where Kolibri's first build lost its players | r1, r5, g2 |
| Travellers walk out after 8 s at the door | Walkouts the player could not prevent are the top complaint about seated service in idle games | g1 |
| The seats lesson fires in only 3 of 6 test runs | Half of new players never learn the second bottleneck | v3-architecture.md, g1 |
| Hires made in the full-screen STAFF panel happen behind it | The most valuable purchase gets the least visible feedback. The tutorial and the NEXT chip both send players to STAFF. | p0, code, g1 |
| The twist stops being the decision after about minute 15 | Waiters, tables and cashiers all hit hard caps (3, 5 and 2), arrivals cap at 19.5 a minute, and throughput levels off near 17 a minute once the kadai is built. After that, growth comes only from raising prices, a few percent per tap. | p0, s_design-first |
| Interstitials after purchases from session 4, 60 s apart | Matches the "after a few days the ads started" uninstall pattern, and earns little at India's ad rates | r2, r3, r5, g3 |
| Never run on a phone | Feel, heat, touch and sound are all unverified | p0 |
| Chai's LEVEL UP gain drops from ₹0.70 to about ₹0.26 a cup at Lv 25, for about the same cost | The button the tutorial teaches pays a third as much per tap afterwards. The ×1.3 milestone and the new dish soften it. | economy_v3.json |

### 6.4 Keep, fix, add, cut

Each group is in priority order. "Smallest version" is the least work that delivers the point.

**Keep**

| Item | Why |
|---|---|
| The first three minutes | Measured and tight; the reference's own reviews call the opening the fun part (r4) |
| The seats-and-waiters twist | It is the genre's core decision, made physical. Idle Restaurant Tycoon proved the structure, and Pizza Ready moved toward it (r5, g1) |
| The NEXT and PROBLEM chip | The genre's red-flag readout in one line; Kolibri stopped quits with on-screen prompts (r1, r5) |
| Honest numbers | Optimisers judge games by them (r2) |
| Rewarded ads only in session 1, free cash first, the VIP offer at most once a session from session 2 | Matches Supercent's three-minute rule and Eatventure's ad contract (r3, r5) |
| The in-world 2-tap hire card | The right way to buy the right thing (g1, r6) |
| The small, offline build | Offline play is praised by name; small size avoids "MB" complaints (r7, g3) |
| The Indian sound and detail | Recognition is what Indian players praise most (r7) |

**Fix before the closed test**

| # | Fix | Smallest version | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Run it on a phone | One build on one 3 to 4 GB Vivo, Redmi or Realme on Android 10 to 12. Record download size, time from icon tap to first sale, frame rate, heat after 20 minutes, and one full session in airplane mode. The phone test sheet in docs/launch/phone-test.md covers most of it. | p0, r7, g3 |
| 2 | Turn interstitials off for the closed test and the soft launch | Set `interstitials_enabled=false` in `config/monetization.cfg`. Read retention first; PLAN.md already says not to read ad revenue in India. If they return later: never within 60 s of a purchase, hire or reward, never on the return screen, at most one every 3 to 5 minutes. | r2, r3, r5, g3 |
| 3 | Make waiting at the door lossless, or slow and readable | Travellers in the door line wait for a seat, and when the line is full, arriving vehicles drive past. ## 7. Claims that failed fact-checking

A second agent checked each sweep. These claims were wrong or out of date. The report above uses the corrections.
- Idle Supermarket Tycoon's managers arrived with version 4 in Feb 2026, not mid-2026, and players read them as a paywall, not as a welcome feature.
- The 2019 to 2020 guides describe its versions 1 to 3. Version 4 removed the money trucks and reshaped the first location.
- Its Google Play price range is $0.99 to $99.99 in the US and ₹89 to ₹8,900 in India, not up to $129.99.
- Pizza Ready's 221M download figure is old. Supercent cites about 400M lifetime installs by Dec 2025.
- The 1.07 cost growth in Pecorella's article is AdVenture Capitalist's lemonade stand, not a universal rate.
- That optional ads are the genre's single most-praised feature is unmeasured. Ads and paywalls are the two leading themes of 1- and 2-star reviews.
- The Indian RAM figures date from 2021, and a 2026 memory-price squeeze has sharply cut entry-level phone shipments. Mobile data plans moved too: ₹349 now buys 2 GB a day for 28 days.
- Eatventure's dates: stackable boosts shipped in Feb 2026 and the Potion Shop in Mar 2025.
- Lessmore is now owned by Miniclip, so its 2022 description as three founders is dated.

Two estimates stay disputed. India's rewarded-ad rate per thousand views ranges from about $0.10 to $2.43 depending on the source. Pizza Ready's India rank in Q1 2026 differs between Tech2 and Sensor Tower.

**Where the three syntheses disagreed, and how this report settled it.** A judge agent scored the syntheses (design-first 34 of 40, market-first 33, player-first 30) and checked them against the notes.
- **What to fix first.** The phone test comes first, because won't-start is the top Hindi complaint and every other test needs a real device.
- **When to show the renovation.** From about minute 3, as Idle Supermarket Tycoon and Eatventure show their next step.
- **What a renovation resets.** Reset levels, staff and tables, but carry dishes and a multiplier over and show it beforehand. The new layout is what keeps the twist alive after the plateau.
- **How much the theme matters.** For installs a lot, for ratings little (section 2.3).
- **Whether size is an edge.** No. It is expected; offline play is the part players praise.
- **LEVEL UP after the tutorial.** Fix the curve itself, not only its label.
