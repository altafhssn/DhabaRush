# Artist brief: Dhaba Rush icon, feature graphic and Munshi-ji key art

Little Tag Art Studios · info@littletagartstudios.com · 2026-10-26

## 1. The game in one paragraph

Dhaba Rush: Highway Idle Tycoon is a mobile idle game (Android first, iOS later) about running a roadside dhaba on an Indian highway. Lorries and buses pull in, travellers sit at charpai tables, waiters carry chai, rotis, lassi and kadai paneer, and the cash counter (galla) rings all day and night under fairy lights. The player levels up four food counters, unlocks 16 dishes and hires waiters, cooks and cashiers. The guide is **Munshi-ji**, the dhaba's warm, slightly nosy accountant. The game is in low-poly 3D, portrait, in English and Hindi, for an Indian soft launch in December 2026.

**Audience:** Indian Android players, roughly 16–35, who play idle, tycoon, cooking and Indian truck or bus games. The art must feel like a fun business game for teens and adults. It must not look like a children's game: Google Play's Families rules apply to listings that appeal to children.

## 2. Why we are commissioning this

Today's icon and feature graphic are made from renders of the game's own 3D models (`export/store/play/icon_512.png`, `feature_graphic.png`). They are honest and readable, but hand-made key art usually earns more taps in the store. We will run a Google Play store listing experiment: the current icon against yours. The winner ships.

## 4. Munshi-ji: character notes

He must match the in-game model closely enough that a player recognises him in the game. The store art must not promise a different game. References: `docs/store/previews/munshi_reference.png` (seven in-game poses), `assets/splash/boot_splash.png`, and screenshot 7 (`export/store/play/shot7_en.png`).

- Round, friendly build; round face, rosy cheeks, big expressive eyes with thick dark brows.
- A big black moustache with curled ends: his signature. It must read at icon size.
- A white Gandhi-style cap (topi): a plain cap only, with no party colours, symbols or flags.
- A maroon Nehru jacket with gold buttons and a small marigold pin, over a cream kurta.
- A red bahi-khata ledger and a yellow pencil tucked behind his right ear.
- Personality: a warm, proud, slightly nosy accountant who calls the player "boss" (सेठ जी in Hindi). Gestures: namaste, a head wobble, slapping the ledger, licking the pencil, sipping chai, palms on cheeks when worried.
- He is an original character. Do not base him on any other game's helper, a real person or a politician.

## 5. Icon directions

- **A (preferred): Munshi-ji's face**, close-up, with a steaming cutting-chai glass. Our procedural version (`export/store/play/icon_512.png`) shows the idea. Make it richer: better lighting, a cleaner silhouette and a livelier expression.
- **B: an object.** A steaming cutting-chai glass, or a chai glass on a dhaba counter with truck-art bunting behind it, on saffron. Our version is `icon_512_b.png`.
- **C (only if A and B fail):** the front of a decorated Indian lorry with a dhaba board.

Readability check: at 48 px on both a light and a dark home screen, inside a circle mask and a squircle mask. `docs/store/previews/icon_readability.png` shows how we test it.

## 6. Style

- Match the game: chunky, rounded, low-poly-friendly shapes and warm light. A painted 2D look or a polished 3D render both work.
- Palette, taken from the game's UI:

| Name | Hex |
|---|---|
| Saffron (icon background, splash) | `#F5A14A`, gradient `#FFC867` → `#F08A2B` |
| Marigold (highlights, titles) | `#FFD23F` |
| Maroon (panels, Munshi-ji's jacket) | `#8F1C3B`, dark `#5C0F24` |
| Peacock teal (STAFF, buttons) | `#0D7D87` |
| Cream (paper, kurta) | `#FFF6E3` |
| Ink (outlines, text) | `#3B2414` |

- Motifs that say "highway dhaba": truck-art borders and bunting, fairy lights and string bulbs, cutting-chai glasses, kulhads, charpais, the tandoor's glow, the "24 घंटे खुला" sign.
- Text: Baloo 2 (OFL, in `assets/fonts/`), the game's font, so Devanagari and Latin match.

## 7. Please avoid

- Other games' characters, layouts or recognisable styles, and any competitor's name.
- Real brands and logos (vehicle makers, fuel companies, food brands), and real people.
- Religious or political symbols, and flag misuse.
- "Pure Veg" or veg-only signs: the dhaba serves egg bhurji.
- Alcohol, tobacco or beedi.
- Toy-like, nursery or "for kids" styling.
- Anything the game does not have: shops, gems, events or characters that are not in it.
- Real cricket teams, leagues or TV channels, and earnings or prize wording ("earn", "win cash", rupee amounts to win). The full list every store image follows is in [README.md §6](README.md#6-store-media-rules-c93).
