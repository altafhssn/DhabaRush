## Shared conventions
- World units = metres. Y up. Character height ≈ 1.0 (traveller models ~1.0–1.15 tall). Camera rotation (−50°, 45°, 0), orthographic, `KEEP_HEIGHT`, gameplay `size` = 9.2 × 1.0 ≈ 9.2.
- Money is stored in the REFERENCE units (start 100, chai price 1.00 + 0.07×(L−1), etc.). The UI shows `₹` × `MONEY_DISPLAY_SCALE` (10) via `Fmt`. Never mix.
- Sim time: `Game.sim_paused` (bool) freezes service timers and traveller path progress (traffic/ambient keep running).
- Fonts: `res://ui/main_font.tres` (Baloo 2 + fallbacks). Colours per spec §4.
- Everything that appears pops (TRANS_BACK) unless the spec says otherwise.

## Addendum 2026-10-20 — BUS STOP ("Buses drop & go")
User decision: lorry bay turnover (~72 s a visit) capped arrivals at ~9–11/min; buses now stop at a BUS STOP instead of parking, so the bays are lorries / tempos / cars only.
- **Flow:** `Sim` calls `Traffic.request_bus(riders)` whenever its arrival account (see the ARRIVAL RAMP addendum below) holds a busload (mean riders × 1 ± `bus_interval_jitter`; first one `bus_first_delay_s` after arrivals open, i.e. in the tutorial only after the first sale and the transfer, never while `hold_spawns`). The bus comes down the −X lane, indicates, eases 0.3 m toward the far kerb and stops at `Layout3.BUS_STOP_X` with its front door (kerb side) at the east end of the lot, by the lay-by (the far shoulder in front of the bays stays clear for reversing lorries): suspension bob + brake dust → `bus_stopped(visit, door, riders)`. `bus_riders_min..max` riders hop down one every 0.4 s and walk along the far shoulder and over the crossing (`Layout3.bus_entry_path`); then everyone in `Sim.bus_line` climbs aboard, one every `bus_board_s` (the line first steps up to the door: `Layout3.bus_board_slot`). When its riders are off and every expected boarder is in (`bus_expect` / `bus_boarded`), the bus honks, puffs exhaust, indicates and pulls away (`bus_departed`; departure manners: see the ARRIVAL RAMP addendum). One bus at the stop at a time; since QA round 1 the next may wait behind it (`Traffic._bus_next`).
- **Bus riders** (`Traveller.bus_rider`): after paying (or giving up for want of a seat) they walk back over the crossing; at the far kerb `Sim.on_traveller_at_bus_stop` gives them a place in the shelter's line (`Traveller.S.BUS_WAIT`, idle with fidgets: glance across the road, bounce on the toes, look about) → `S.BOARDING` → gone.
