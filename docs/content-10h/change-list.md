3 Nov 2026. This is the approval list for the 10-hour content design. Evidence for every item is in [report.md](report.md); section numbers below point there. **There is no build plan yet: the owner approves this list first.** IDs are stable: a later plan, code comments, QA logs and reports will refer to them.

**Phase labels** (cost = planned packages / with fixes and gates / new art files / download / new English msgids, each needing Hindi later; all from report §7):
- **Pre-launch**: phase 1, built before the soft launch. Cost: 12 / 22-24 / ~31 / ~0.45-0.85 MB / ~171.
- **Update 1 (post-data)**: phase 2, the first update, after the D1-D7 soft-launch data. Cost: 7.5 / 15-17 / 13-16 / ~0.4-0.7 MB / ~72-87 (+0.5-1 package only if the hotel roof needs a `dhaba_v3.glb` rebuild, X6.1).

- **Reserve (post-data)**: only if the soft-launch data shows a wall after the content.

What each phase gives (report §2.3, §8): phase 1 alone gives a typical player (5 sessions of 8 minutes a day) something new on every one of days 1-15, the first 10 in-app hours; the authored story ends on day 1 22:00 (bot: 2:33:23). Update 1 moves the story to day 2 11:30 (bot 3:39:00); update 2 to day 2 22:01 (bot 6:18:09). Those are fresh installs. Bar B ("something affordable about every 5 minutes") holds inside the story only: in phase 1's tail about half of a typical player's sessions buy nothing that raises takings (report §5.2; XD21). For players who already have the game (report §2.3, §8; XD18):
- **Update 1**, for a typical phase 1 save on day 7 (46.17 Cr in hand, 341 minutes of takings): it adds a stage to every highway for new installs, but a player who has already moved gets only the hotel of the highway they are on (moves are one-way, `game.gd:5262-5283`), plus 7 Izzat for each hotel left behind (X6.7). That one hotel is done in 11 s of play (a fresh install needs 7:57). Decor then lasts about a week if the update lands on day 7 (the 7th item on day 14), and one session from day 15 (runs LIVE and LIVE10, report §2.3).
- **Update 2**, for a typical phase 2 save on day 16 (117.11 Cr, 680 minutes of takings): NH 66 and NH 16 with both hotels are done in 17 s of play (a fresh install needs 21:16). Festivals, counter looks and stars remain.

## Ground rules for every builder

- **No APK or iOS export without the owner's approval of that build.** No git: no commits, no pushes. Never run Godot against the real project or the real save: use copies in the QA harness slots (`savecheck.sh` before and after).
- **Old saves must load and must never be wiped**: the 0.3.7 fixtures, mid-tutorial saves, saves at NH 48, and saves with a `sys` block. **Never bump `SAVE_VERSION`** (`game.gd:5908-5910`: a new version starts fresh and the autosave overwrites the old file). New keys are optional with defaults; new state lives in `sys` modules (`game.gd:414-420`). Never rename a dish key. Ship highways forward only, and a0 keeps an unknown highway id instead of loading it at NH 44 (`game.gd:5918-5928`; X4.1).
- **Hindi is frozen until the owner finalises the English**: no edits to `i18n/`, to Hindi docs, or to the Hindi fields in data (`name_hi`, `sign_hi` in `data/economy_v3.json`). A new highway's `name_hi` and `sign_hi` stay "" until the Hindi pass (report §7). Count every new English msgid and add it to the Hindi backlog. English always comes first; no Hindi-only labels.
- **The tutorial keeps its beats and timings.** No award, star, toast or pop-up before `is_tutorial_done()`.
- **Economy numbers live in `data/economy_v3.json`.** Check pacing with `tools/sim/pacing_v3.py` (the PACE package adds its sessions and calendar mode and mirrors the new mechanics there); bar A is checked with `pacing_v3.py --sessions` on the tuned data (X1.5). The design prototype `tools/sim/content10h.py` with `tools/sim/content10h/design_final.json` is for design only, never used by the game or the gates. Use `/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/Resources/5.2/python/bin/python3.13 -I -B`.
- **The research rules:** no paid gates or timers; something affordable about every 3-5 minutes; something new regularly; lengthen with new stages and goals, never by raising the L100 gate or the L300 cap and never by pure cost inflation; ads opt-in and rewarded only (interstitials off); never add paid manager cards, research tokens, timers on instant actions, new chain links (cleaners, wages, staff who quit), boosts that bring more travellers than free seats, offer stacks, or a premium currency with nothing to buy; Izzat is never sold; keep downloads small; no "Pure Veg" or "Vaishno" branding; no beef, pork or alcohol (and no bhang or zarda); no India map; nothing like betting or prediction (cricket night included); town names, never brands; religious places, figures and objects (holy sites, deities and idols, temple offerings, prayer flags, chariots) are scenery or travel context only, never rewards or jokes (highways §11.6).
