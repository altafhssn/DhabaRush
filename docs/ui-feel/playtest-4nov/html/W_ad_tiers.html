W5-W9 · the ad tiers in the world
Every paid ad leaves something you can see in the dhaba or on the road, plus a short line from the travellers it brought.
Drawing, not final art
W5 · Newspaper ad
A paper stack lands on galla 1 with a thwack; Munna reads it between bills (new read pose). Diners now sometimes read one too. Travellers at the gate: 1 in 6 say Saw your ad! for 5 min.
W6 · FM radio jingle
A wooden radio joins galla 1's shelf (not G16's transistor: OD-W4). Every ~3 min a 4-note jingle and three notes float up; Pinky sways. Lorries that turn in show notes from the cab; gate travellers say Heard on FM!
W7 · Cable TV ad
The TV plays the dhaba's own 5 s spot (a shader, no words) every ~90 s, by day too. Diners in view turn their heads to it; one says That's us! The pending G6 meter covers the TV at home.
W8 · Highway boards
No tall poles: a 6.5 m-high board stands about 450 px up the screen and would hide the lot. Instead the roadside boards the camera already sees take your ad: the 3 painted wall ads, a lit bus-shelter poster, a panel under TRUCK PARKING.
W9 · Campaign (short)
While it runs: a loudspeaker auto with posters drives past every ~40 s playing the jingle, and a cloth banner hangs on the front wall. Gate travellers turn to look; a loudspeaker icon, no words.
Rules for all five
Each tier keeps its object in the world for good. Its gate line: 1 in 6 entering travellers for 5 min after the buy, then 1 in 30, at most one every 8 s, inside the global cap of 2 bubbles on screen. The buy itself: the camera shows the object (2.5 s hold), then flies back. No new characters. Only the auto adds a vehicle, while the campaign runs.
