## 1. The idea

The interface is Munshi-ji's own book-keeping: every panel is a page of his bahi-khata, bound in red cloth, written in blue-black ink, closed with brass, and marked with rubber stamps. The dialogue box already looks like this (`dialogue_box.gd:67-76`), so the look grows the one piece of today's UI that already reads as "dhaba" until the whole interface is one family.

**Where it fits best.**
- The Daftar. BUILD is already "daftar" and STATS is already "hisaab". As a ledger, STATS becomes a calm two-column account (`K/png/05_stats.png`).
- The counter sheets as one ledger page per counter, with the eight-fold "sal" columns as the guides between COOKS, LEVEL UP and SPEED (`K/png/03_sheet_chai.png`).
- WELCOME BACK as the day's hisaab: a ledger total with a double rule (`K/png/06_welcome_back.png`).
- Settings, consent, quit and the STAFF register (not mocked; same parts).
- The task strip as a chit, and states as rubber stamps (built, done, NEW).
- It fits rewards least well. See section 12.

**Separation from the warm world.** Paper alone would sink by day. Against the world behind the top HUD band (`BG/mid/t009.0.png`, x 0-720, y 100-500), measured with `PY K/work/sep.py`:
- The world's median luminance is 0.323 (p25 0.190, p75 0.645).
- Paper is 2.41:1 against that median, and about 1.3:1 against its bright quarter (sand roofs, cream walls).
- The cloth binding is 3.04:1 against the median. So by day the 6-canvas-px cloth edge and the drop shadow do the separating, not the paper.
- At night (`BG/night/t004.0.png`) it flips: lamp-lit paper is 6.74:1 against the median and the cloth almost vanishes (1.09:1). So at night the paper and its warm glow separate the HUD.
- Rule that follows: never thin the cloth binding below 6 canvas px, and never drop the shadow by day.

## 4. Type

**Faces** (all SIL OFL with Devanagari and Latin, from Google Fonts; licence and scripts checked from METADATA in D5 §4 and its verification):
- **Baloo 2** (already shipped, `assets/fonts/Baloo2.ttf`, 683,200 bytes) for everything that is not a title: labels, body, buttons and every number. Numbers switch on tabular figures (`tnum`), so cash and columns stop jiggling. The font has `tnum` (D5 §4); the game never enables it today.
- **Eczar ExtraBold (800)** for titles only: panel titles, sheet titles, dish and counter names, the popup title and the rubber-stamp word. It is a printed-almanac serif, which is the "book" voice. Its Devanagari is strong (`K/png/09_hindi.png`: "चाय काउंटर", "डीज़ल चाय", "नान").
- **Kalam Bold (700)** for Munshi-ji's own hand only: the dialogue box, his chips, and the "daftar" / "hisaab" notes. Never for prices, buttons or numbers (D5 §3.2).
- One weight each of the two new faces. Size: Kalam Regular is 427,360 bytes (D5 §4); Kalam Bold and Eczar ExtraBold were not measured (estimate 0.3-0.5 MB each, unverified). Godot's text server shapes Devanagari for both; a missing conjunct would fall back to Baloo 2 mid-word (unverified, D1 §5.4).

**Scale: 6 sizes, floor 14 canvas px.**

**Rules.**
- Line pitch follows Baloo 2's own box (K1): about 1.3 x the size for wrapped text (25 px at 19 px), not 34 px. The mockups set `line-height: 1.3`.
- Dark text on light faces everywhere. No outlines.
- No faked italics. "/min" is upright; "daftar" and "hisaab" are in Kalam, a real hand, not a 0.2 shear (`ui_kit.gd:121-122` today).
- One title per screen. The BUILD and STATS info entries lose their repeated heading (`K/png/04_build_daftar.png`, `K/png/05_stats.png`).
- Latin caps labels get +0.04-0.06 em letter-spacing. Devanagari gets none, or its headline breaks (checked in `K/png/09_hindi.png` on "मेन्यू में नया" and the "नया" stamp).

## 5. The parts, and how to build them in Godot

**How 2x art is drawn.** All art is authored at 2x (1 canvas px = 2 texture px), as D1 §5.4 asks for 1.5x phones. A plain StyleBoxTexture draws its nine-slice margins 1:1 in canvas units, so 2x art would draw twice too big. The proposal: one scripted style box, `StyleBoxKhata extends StyleBox`, that overrides `_draw(to_canvas_item, rect)`. It sets `RenderingServer.canvas_item_add_set_transform(item, Transform2D().scaled(Vector2(0.5, 0.5)))`, calls `canvas_item_add_nine_patch` with the 2x texture over `rect` doubled, then resets the transform. Content margins stay in canvas px. Textures import as VRAM Compressed with mipmaps, so stitches do not shimmer at 0.5x. **This is untested in Godot 4.7.** Build the cash plate first and check it on a phone before making the other 29 textures. Fallback: a NinePatchRect at double size inside a Control scaled 0.5.

## 6. Icons

**Style rules** (`K/html/khata.js`, 48-unit grid):
- Objects from a highway dhaba, not stock glyphs. STAFF is a worker with a gamchha, not a two-person group. BUILD is a brick and a mason's karni, not a hammer and axe. STATS is the bahi-khata itself, not a bar chart. Settings is a brass cog with ink edges (a cog stays, because players look for it).
- One brown-black ink outline, 2.4 units (about 3 canvas px at the 64 px size). Flat fills from the world palette. No gradients, no gloss, no white sticker outline.
- On paper, an icon sits in a red-ink stamp ring.

## 12. The direction's question, answered honestly
